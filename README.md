# The Idea Factory

The Idea Factory is a **free, public database** that contains **all my ideas**. 

It currently contains only **~700 unique ideas**. By the time I'm done migrating all my unused ideas (for now), it will likely contain 1000 ideas.

**Why?** I have way too many ideas (at least 20 a day). I do _not_ have enough time/resources/skill to create 99% of them.

So I decided to give them to others! As inspiration, a starting point, or your next big project when you're stuck.

**Where?** The database is currently hosted for free at [Factory of Ideas (Cloudflare Pages)](https://factory-of-ideas.pages.dev). 

Once I create an actual UI theme (instead of AI placeholders) and finish migrating all my ideas, I will register a proper paid domain for it.

**Why (Again)?** The landing page will answer all your questions. A summary:

* No, these are not just my worst ideas. (Each idea is something I was really excited about once, something novel and interesting, but also out of my reach for whatever reason.)
* Yes, everyone is free to use any part of it for anything. (I think everything should be open and free in general. I will never ever come after someone over copyright or something similar.)
* No, this isn't useless, not even in the age of AI. (There are actually many people great at _executing_ who struggle to come up with _ideas_. Go visit any subreddit, like r/Writing, and see how many people ask others for ideas, because they love writing books but just don't know _what_ to write.)
* Yes, the ideas are very diverse. (They can include images, audio, video. Some are just one paragraph, some are massive text files with details. They span from books, to games, to music, to software/tools, and more.)

I _might_ one day pick one of these ideas myself, if the opportunity suddenly presents itself to make it. But I only put ideas on there of which I deem the likelihood of that ever happening near 0%. Even so, I don't care if others already executed the idea in the meantime, as I will probably end up somewhere else entirely with my own project.

**What now?**

Be inspired! 

Go forth and make cool stuff! 

Show me what you made with my ideas!
