A _One Button Game_: play with multiple people behind the same screen/keyboard. Every player gets exactly one button.

Some people are a TORCH/CANDLE/LANTERN. Their light is **only on while they hold their button**. 

The other people are ACTORS (who actually _do stuff_). They can move all the time, but only **do their thing IN THE DARKNESS**. 

As such, you have to work together and constantly alternate 

* See stuff
* Do stuff (remembering what you saw and where) 
* See stuff
* Do stuff
* And so forth 

_Somewhat inspired by <https://hot-diggity-dog.itch.io/substratum>. Although in that game you SHOOT PROJECTILES which are the light you see, which is ALSO COOL._
