Basically _Hide & Seek_, but as a _One Button Game_. 

With a single button per player, it's hard to move around. That's why, on purpose, the movement (of hiders) is pretty much _silly and random_. Just press your button and ... see where you end up. This makes hiding actually quite difficult, no matter what the arenas look like, no matter how much time the hiders get.

I envisioned the look and movement a bit like how in _LEGO Voyagers_ your single-block characters roll and stick to stuff.
