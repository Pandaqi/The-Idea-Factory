A _One Button Game_: multiple players behind the same screen, each one only has a single button.

Can we do a "creative/sandbox" one button game, like Minecraft? 

Maybe you're **decorating a house**. Every player gets their own floor. Possible things to place scroll past (like a news ticker), and you place whatever is below your cursor when you press the button.
