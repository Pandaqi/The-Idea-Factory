The screen is split into *many* different views of the same map. 

They are scrambled, making it hard to figure out the _real_ layout of the world and where everything is. 

This requires a world that is absolutely *huge*, so you *need* the many views to keep track of what's happening. A bit like a grid of security cameras?

What's the goal? You need to track/find certain people and elements, then **press/hold your button at the right time**.

* Each player gets their own missions to follow, track, and complete. 
  * This is as simple as "when the RED BLOB is in front of the house, hold your button for 1 second"
  * Or "Make sure the camera in the top left sees the briefcase at some point"
* After each completed mission, the views change position again? Is there some OTHER way in which players have individual control over individual views?
