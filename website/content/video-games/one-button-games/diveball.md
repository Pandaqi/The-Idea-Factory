A _One Button Game_ where you play with multiple people behind the same screen, and each player gets exactly one button.

You play a simple ball sport/game, trying to get that ball into a goal.

* **Pressing** the button will make you _dive_ towards the ball.
* **Holding** the button will charge you up; once released, you dive _away_ from the ball.

> Potentially Better Idea: releasing makes you dive away from WHERE THE BALL **WAS** when you started charging!
