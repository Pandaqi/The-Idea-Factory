A _One Button Game_: you play with multiple people behind the same screen, each player gets exactly one button.

2D side-view arena. You play a simple soccer game, or maybe just "carry objects X to Y".

Here's the trick:

> **All the "platforms" are CONVEYOR BELTS.** 

That's how you move around automatically without needing a button for it! When your character lands on the platform, it's simply taken with it, following the automatic conveyor belt. (As such, the idea might be named _Conveyor Beltball_ too.)

What does your one button do?

* BUTTON PRESS = switch the direction of your current conveyor belt?
* BUTTON HOLD AND RELEASE = jump (longer hold = bigger jump; you carry momentum from belt into that jump)

A creative way to move around a simple arena, perhaps playing a sport or just trying to stay alive.
