A silly game where everyone is a soldier bouncing around, struggling to aim their gigantic gung.

## Objective

Kill all enemies. Last one standing.

## Input/Action

You are a **circular soldier** with a **weapon pointing a certain direction**.

You randomly bounce around through the level. (Realistic physics, you're literally a ball.)

You have **one button:**

* When held, you counter-rotate. (So whatever way you were rotating when you press the button, you now accelerate in the other direction.)
* When released, you shoot.

## Map

Just a basic arena with some blacks, hiding spots, jumping spots.

By hitting stuff with the right side of your body (or shooting other stuff), you can also *control your movement*.

## Multiplayer

Everyone their own soldier. Allow dropping in/out on a whim.
