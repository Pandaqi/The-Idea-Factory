The idea of "move by click/dragging mouse" just feels really simple and a foundation for a one button game. For example, I was inspired by [Bufo Jump](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4175980/Bufo_Jump/).

How do we make that a _One Button Game_?

* Press your button to "start drag"
* Your marker will automatically move forward in the chosen direction, a few pixels at a time.
* Press your button again to "stop drag" => now your player jumps to this new location.

Now players could be bunnies and you're trying to hop over things, land on islands (instead of the water), and collect carrots that way.
