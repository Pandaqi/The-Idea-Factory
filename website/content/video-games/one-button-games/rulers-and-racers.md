Grid-based racing.

Each turn several *options* appear at the bottom of your screen. 

> For example: "Turn Left Twice" or "Go Forward Once"

You tap the one you want. (Or select it with the keys on keyboard.)

The correct one is always there. (Because it's grid-based, I *know* at least one direction in which you can surely go.)

It selects one by default, at random. If you do nothing *within the time limit*, it picks that one.

## As a One Button Game

You're steering that car TOGETHER.

There are multiple options (turn left, turn right, double speed ahead, etc), in a randomized order. (With button visualizers below for indication as to what key to press.)

So players need to very quickly see what THEY are and communicate **WHO should press their button to decide their next action**.
