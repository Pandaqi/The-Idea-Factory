A One Button Game called "We Golf Together" (like rama lama lama ka dinga da dinga dong).

You're all controlling the **same person** trying to play (mini) golf. Each input simply does a different thing.

* One determines swing power/speed.
* One determines angle/movement.
* One determines the placement of the ball.
* Etcetera

You might do these things _simultaneously_ ... or in _tandem_. (So whatever stupid thing the player before you did, like setting a really strange angle for your swing, you're now stuck with that.)

_I think this is a fine idea, but beyond a silly party gimmick I don't see much educational value (for my online store) or longevity here. An educational golf game should make each player golf on their own and give accurate feedback about angles and stuff to actually teach that._
