I once had this idea of a **slot machine** game, but as a One Button Game, perhaps for my educational online store.

This was inspired by seeing this game: <https://store.steampowered.com/app/3631290/Slots__Daggers/>

However, the more I thought about it, the less it made sense for my specific purposes.

* Yes, it is "one button" to stop the slot machine. But it's basically random---it's not the _point_ of such games.
* The point is that the randomness from the slot machine decides your next strategic actions/moves or what you can buy in the tech tree or whatever.
* That entire point would be gone in a simplified OBG or educational game (about probability/matching) ...

So what would be left? "Press your button to stop the slot machine. Score based on whatever your random result is. Repeat." Not that interesting or educational, is it?
