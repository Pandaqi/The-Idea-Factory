A _One Button Game_ that you play with multiple people behind the same device, and each player gets exactly one button.

* There's one "bomb" that someone is holding at the start. 
* All you can do is press your button to aim + shoot, and hope the bomb lands with another player.
* (If you miss, it just returns to you.)
* In this way, the bomb will constantly be changing hands.

> **When the timer runs out, you DON'T want to be the one holding it. Because it means you lose.**
