A _One Button Game_: multiple players behind the same screen, each one only has a single button.

You're all walking a stairway together. (Left to right, higher and higher until the top).

**Your button** just makes you **take the next step**. 

Reach the top with the _best score_, not necessarily first. 

(A kind of "smell the flowers" metaphor in there?) 

This mechanic is _so simple_ because the _actual game_ will be more complex. Like, maybe you're collecting letters or numbers, maybe there's some even harder mechanics going on. **That will be the educational component of this one.**
