You're all just birds sitting on the same rope-y wire. 

**You're trying NOT to fall off.**

It's a _One Button Game_: play with multiple people behind the same screen, each person has only one button.

**Holding** your button will make you hold onto the string (or another bird?), but you have very limited grip strength/duration.

**Pressing** your button will make you do a little hop in whatever direction you're facing/leaning.

_What does "falling off" mean in a 2D side-view?_ 

* Maybe it just means going off-screen (at the top), or falling off the _sides_ (extreme points of the rope).
* The swinging of the rope, its elasticity, hitting you, will generate massive forces that will dislocate you over time.
