This is a _One Button Game_. Many players can join behind the same device/keyboard; each player only gets one unique button and that's it.

All players are **static scarecrows**. You never move from your spot. The map and your positions are randomly generated at the start.

Your button PRESS simply creates a kind of "blast". Anything within the radius is blown away (most likely, you scared a bunch of crows away, it's in the job description).

By timing your blasts, and when you DON'T do it, you should be able to corral things to the right spot.

@IDEA: You can change the range/angle of your blasts through HOLDING or POWERUPS or whatever.

@NOTE: This works best with 3/4+ players. With fewer players you should probably be able to switch between multiple scarecrows, or SOMETHING should help you out and give more control.
