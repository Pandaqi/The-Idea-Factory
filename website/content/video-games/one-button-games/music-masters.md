Something inspired by this: <https://www.reddit.com/r/godot/comments/1pamgv1/i_created_a_quantizerconductor_for_my_game_that/>

* Bumping into things creates sounds
* The **closer** you get those sounds to the **8th beats** (or whatever) of the music, the **better**.
* (Additionally, if so, **snap** them to the nearest 8th note perfectly to make it sound nice.)

In other words, players must move in sync with the beat or risk losing.
