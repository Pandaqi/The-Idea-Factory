You play volleyball, 2D side view.

But everyone controls a **vertical stack of multiple (tiny) people.**

Each player gets one button. 

> **When pressed, your entire stack jumps up at once** (then automatically falls down due to gravity).

So it's like foosball (table soccer), but made vertical and volleyball!

I envisioned a kind of Totem, Maya/Aztec, Mesoamerican visual style for this. (The stack of tiny people _is_ a totem, for example.)
