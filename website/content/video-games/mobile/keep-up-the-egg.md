A _local multiplayer_ game, 2D side-view, single-screen (manual?) level design. A very simple platformer layout.

Players are moving/jumping around **while eggs fall from the sky**, and they obviously **may not hit the floor**. Keep them up, keep them from breaking, as long as possible.

_Even though this is just a sliver of an idea, I marked it quite high in terms of potential because, well, I think this simple idea could actually sustain a full commercial game._
