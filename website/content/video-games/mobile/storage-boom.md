You see two (storage) rooms side by side. (Players are also supposed to sit side by side on the same side of the screen?)

**Stuff falls from the sky** (or appears in the center/appears somehow). You have to select some yourself, while others have to be **kicked/thrown/passed to the other side**.

Or maybe you just have two "robot arms" and two buttons to press. 

* One of them pushes _sideways_ to the other side (to kick packages to the other player).
* One of them pushes _up_ (or opens a hole, or out of the screen, or whatever) for packages that YOU want to collect.
