A quick game about capturing a creature of whom you can see the next few moves.

## Goal

You win when the creature has no more moves

You lose when you have no more moves / you share the space with the creature

## Gameplay

You fly around a grid. When you press a fence, it places one in the direction you're looking.

There's a grid of commands/arrows at the bottom. Every X seconds, it refreshes and **the creature executes the first one**. (As such, this is the "future path" of the creature)

When the creature bumps into a fence (or _blocked_ otherwise), you are rewarded with a new one in your inventory.

You can go through fences; the creature can not.
