A game using **bunnyhop movement**.

* Press+hold to power up
* The more power used, the higher/further you hop

Nothing else happens automatically. For example, to turn around, hit something while in the air, and you bounce away.

**Mostly meant as a cooperative game:** one player is long-ear, the other is two-teeth.

Two screens, next to each other. The world is moving up. Use bunnyhop jumps to get as high as possible.

You help each other by grabbing stuff meant for the other player.

**In single player, you control both bunnies?** => It simply checks which half (left or right) of the screen you touch. If two-player, though, it increases the difficulty and intensity, so you really *need* two players.

**IDEA:** One bunny is *underground*. Making their hole, digging into the earth. The other is above ground, collecting food, fleeing from danger.
