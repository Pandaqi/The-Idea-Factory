A grid of squares. Each square **cycles through several options** (on a timer). For example,

> Move left/right/up/down. Get some power/ingredient. Shoot. Whatever.

* Each player can only press _one button_. => This will look like an actual button, but they can actually just press that _entire area_ of the device.
* Which, when pressed, does the thing your _current square does_. (Example: your square currently shows "MOVE LEFT". If you press the button now, you move to the left.) 

Could be competitive, but the easiest is always cooperative (to allow solo mode).
