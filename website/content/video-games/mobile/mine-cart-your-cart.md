## Goal

Reach the highest score / stay alive as long as possible.

You lose when time runs out / you've collected too many bad things.

## Gameplay

There are (mine) carts at the bottom of the screen.

By pressing a **specific cart**/area/button, the connected (= same-color) cart **moves in that direction**.
* There is some momentum here (they take a while to speed up and slow down over time), but not much. Should be snappy.
* They flip around automatically when hitting the wall. (Carts can overlap otherwise?)

Stuff falls from the top. You need to make sure **the right thing falls into the right cart**.
* COLORED = the main one, just get it in the right color cart
* BOMB = always bad
* RAINBOW CART = accepts everything (though make sure you won't just use THAT all the time)

## Local Multiplayer?

With **2 Players**, 

* Either the buttons to move each cart LEFT/RIGHT (independently) are spread between the players.
* Or each cart simply has a single button, and only one player can move that one color
