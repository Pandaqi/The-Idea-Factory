A game where you're paired with one or more _very simple, consistent AIs_. 

For example,

* BOT = "Always takes 1 step left, 1 step right, then attacks"
* BOT = "Always defends and runs as far away as possible from everything"
* BOT = "Always walks forward, never attacks"

The AIs aren't smart or good---it's _your job_ to make the most of the abilities they do have. 

The goal would simply be to fulfill missions or defend some tower. You can't do it alone, you _need_ your twenty robot friends, but they're obviously only going to do the one thing they were programmed to do.

This also makes multiplayer very easy. More players? More robot friends + replace some bots with human players.
