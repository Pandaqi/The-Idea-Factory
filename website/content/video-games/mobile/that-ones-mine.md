A fast game about claiming properties ( = homes/buildings/locations) on a shared map before their value drops, with local multiplayer potential.

## Gameplay

Claim houses/properties/squares on the map.

The map is a grid of squares that constantly show different numbers, rising in value, dropping, etcetera. You can _claim_ the current value by paying the associated price from your personal money.

To do so, **start a swipe** from your area/quadrant, and **end it on the property you wanted**.

> Each player probably just gets their money total + a money bag icon in their corner.

This allows multiplayer play on the same phone quite easily.

Each house has a specific _type_. Based on what players buy (and don't buy), the values and speed of change of each type changes.
* Example: If people have bought a lot of TURTLE homes, then any other turtle home shoots up in value.
* Example: If a CAT home disappeared completely without anyone buying it, then other cat homes also reduce in value.

## Objective

Be the first to acquire 1000 money!
