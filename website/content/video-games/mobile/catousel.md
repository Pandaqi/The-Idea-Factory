## Goal

Shoot/catapult all the objects into the right slots.

You only have X time to shoot the next one. If you fail to do so, a random object/projectile is removed. Losing too many is death.

## Gameplay

* The level is filled with _rotating circles_ (or something else that rotates with clearly defined sides), with _different options_ on each side. (Square, triangle, circle, etcetera.)
* At the bottom are 1--3 options of things to catapult.
* Swipe (starting from an option) to shoot it in that direction.
* It is only "accepted" if it hits the matching image on the side of those circles. (Otherwise, after a few bounces, it will fall down and be lost.)
