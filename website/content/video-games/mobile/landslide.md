Based on those "LunarLander" demo projects they always make to test AI algorithms.

## Gameplay

* Rockets/Planes/whatever appears from the sky, slowly falling down.
* Each has a specific _target destination_ near the bottom. (This is color/type/pattern-coded.)
* You must move them around to make sure they **land on their target**!

## Input

* Click something to select it. (They are also mapped to your number keys.)
* Then you can turn it left, turn it right, or throttle.
