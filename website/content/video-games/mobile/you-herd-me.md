Also known as the **blow-away-game**. Its mechanic could be used for other things too.

## Goal

Bring all the sheep to the right locations.

## Gameplay

You can **tap the screen to "blow away" any nearby sheep.**

This can be solo, cooperative or 1v1 (with your own separated sides).

Possibly this is a limited resource, to prevent just mindlessly tapping all the time. (It refills over time + sheep/taps can get more.)

> YES! Powerups appear that you must collect with a sheep, which give strategy and change what you can do. => You must not only shepherd sheep, but also on a trajectory that makes them grab the most powerups along the way!

Tapping with your finger might be fine too, but is less interesting. Only for weak powerups?

When does it end?

* If playing alone, you're against a timer OR you're not allowed to "lose" too many sheep (to dangers or something).
* If playing versus, it's about who can place all the sheep where they need to be _first_.
