You are a **fisher** sitting in the **corner of the screen**. (This allows up to 4 players to play on the same device.)

* By tapping your character ( + swiping/aiming/loading) you can keep **shooting fish hooks into the playing field**.
* You obviously want to hook the good things for yourself, and not the bad things.
* And player hooks interact with each other, so you can collide into each other and ruin another player's attempt to grab a fish.

When time runs out, or somebody reaches X score, the game ends. Highest score wins.

If playing alone, your fisher can change corners during the game? It _automatically_ hops to the next corner every 5 seconds?

We can make fishing a multi-step process.

* First tap = for aiming
* Second tap = for power

Or we can simply make it a single swipe or a single tap.
