Recreate the game Asteroids, but **flying _two_ ships** (or allowing _two_ players on the same device).

Should probably do something like this:

* Each device has _three_ controls: left/right, button to accelerate, button to shoot.
* When playing alone, you simply have these laid out nicely side by side.
* When playing duo, there are two ships and two sets of controls. 

> You get one or two controls from the _other_ ship!
