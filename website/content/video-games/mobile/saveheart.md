Two players sit opposite each other: a 1v1 battle. Each starts with a **big heart at their backline to protect**.

* You have an "inventory" of towers/defenders/units that starts with just 1.
* By tapping any location you can place your current frontmost unit there.
* Each unit has a _timer_. It remains fixed in place until it's done, then it returns to the _end_ of your inventory.
* Units shoot arrows/projectiles all around + soak up attacks from the enemy.
	* Hit precious stuff, or stuff in the environment, to get more money for more towers.
	* Hit the heart enough times to win the game.

Probably makes more sense if the _heart wanders of its own accord_, right? (And its movement might or might not be shown ahead of time, like a path or destination arrow.)
