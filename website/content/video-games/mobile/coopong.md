You're playing pong 1v1 (2 players opposite device). But it's _cooperative_.

Objects/powerups appear in the _center_. They also have a color.

* When the ball **touches your paddle**, it gets your color (and returns to the other side).
* Now the ball can _only_ pick up **powerups with a matching color**.
* Obviously, there are some powerups you never want, and some balls should just be let go---off screen---to remove them.
