A very simple game of (beach)volleyball. 

* You look from a 3/4 view (near isometric)
* There's a net and two teams on either side.
* Players are just circles ("balloons").
* Simply **hit the ball to bounce it back.** (Physics engine takes care of all this.)
* At most X bounces are allowed before it has to move to the other side. 
* If it ever hits the floor, the opponent gets a point.

Players are heavy, the ball is light, that's how you can get it to bounce off of your head quite rapidly. (Because _F = ma_. The same force will cause a greater acceleration in the lower mass ball.)

You can walk around and jump, of course. But by **holding the jump button** you can start floating like a balloon. That's where the name of this idea comes from.

* This approximates the idea of boosting from _Rocket League_, allowing more freedom in the air and more plays.
* But it is much simpler and more restrained, suitable for a simple party game for everyone.
