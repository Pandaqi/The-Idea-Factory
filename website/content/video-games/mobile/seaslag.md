A local multiplayer version of **Battleships**, to be played on the same phone.

You must place some divider (a folded up paper) halfway your phone screen. Both players get a secret base/layout and take turns shooting at each other (or do so simultaneously). 

**Take down all opponent ships to win!**

You'd need a way to pick which square or which action _without_ pressing the actual location, otherwise you give it away.

You could also use your hands to shield your part of the map, of course. But that would require a _big_ part of the center to not register any taps or clicks or touches.
