## Goal

Stay alive as long as possible.

## Gameplay

A random "conveyor belt" is generated. (Same algorithm as my _Zoo Escape_ prototype; it snakes across the entire screen, starting from outside the screen.)

Random colored packages appear and move along it (from start->finish)

Next to the belt are "pusher" (or blower or magnet) machines. When you tap them, they'll blow packages **of the same color** off of the belt.

* Doing so gives you points (and removes the package)
* If a package ever reaches the end ( = you failed to deliver it), you lose a life
