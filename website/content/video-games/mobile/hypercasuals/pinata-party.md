There's a Pinata in the center of the screen, with a certain unique shape.

**Swipe the screen to hit it.** (Touch or mouse, of course.)

* Dots/sticks appear in the corner.
* When you swipe, it moves the other end of the stick with your finger, to simulate a physical swinging object.
* This way, you need to check the possible locations and find the right direction for a swipe.
* Failsafes: don't allow swipes to start within the pinata, or when too far away from a dot.

In general, hitting it at top speed will deal more damage and give more points.

Otherwise, there are objectives:

* Turn it around
* Hit very specific spots
* Make the candy land in a target location.
