You have a **group of fans always following you**, always trying to get near.

You can move around. 

All you have to do, is move around in such a way that you **lose the bad fans**, and perhaps **keep the good fans**.

There are locations on the map where you have to be. You have quite some time to get there, but the people you have with you *once there* determines if you get points, or a penalty, or whatever happens.

## Direct Inspiration

- **Neon War:** very simple yet effective idea. Can easily become *multiplayer*.
- **99 Balls:** that original game (asteroids? Bricks?), but with nice twists to make it modern and more varied
