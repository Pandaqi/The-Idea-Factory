Silly physics walking seems to do well as a hypercasual game.

So, get inspired by

* That unicycle game. 
* Kori walk. 
* The one where the bird can only hop left/right (although I found that a bit *too* annoying).

Theme it around something with **"school" or "job"**. It's universal. Same with animals, candy/food, well-known sports and activities.
