A vertical level. Walls left and right, sometimes obstacles/walls/floors in the center.

You're just a blob. An arrow rotates around you.

- Touch = arrow stops
- Hold = arrow fills up
- Release = jump in the direction of the arrow, at the given speed

You stick to most things.

The idea is to get as *high* as possible, as *fast* as possible.

- Quicker jumps give bonus points?
- The floor is slowly rising. (But you can get a headstart on that, so it's not a *constant* pressure to be fast.)
- The longer you wait, the lower your *maximum* power becomes, and the *slower* the arrow rotates. (This also helps with aiming, so waiting longer can be beneficial.)
