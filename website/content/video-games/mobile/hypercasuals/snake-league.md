Play a game of pong (or some other sports-like thing) ... but you're *snakes* that *grow* over time. 

The usual rules of snake apply: your body grows behind you, and you can't touch it.

Snakes move over a grid, the ball does not.

To simplify the game, you **automatically move forward** and the grid shows **arrows**. When you tap the screen, you simply rotate left/right in the direction of your current arrow.

You also *shrink* automatically?

So you can't constantly defend your own goal, you need to get out of your way to eat something extra and stay at a good size.
