The map shows a bunch of animals in cages. When you tap/press one, it **opens their gate**, and they will automatically walk to the furthest edge of the screen ("out of the zoo").

You must time this properly, so they don't get hit by dangers between their cage and the goal on the other side!

Animals are *cute*. They'd also be a pain to draw + animate ...

Alternative name: The Great Animal Escape.
