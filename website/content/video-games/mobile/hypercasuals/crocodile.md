Bugs are flying overhead. Crocodiles are in the water that shoot up once in a while.

Obviously, the crocodiles want to eat the bugs.

You can play the **crocodile** or the **bug**.

- Use flappy-bird movement if you're a bug. (And things in the air can turn you around or change things.) It'd be about nimble movement and dodging crocodiles.
- With the crocodiles, you merely *select* (and perhaps "power up") the one you want and then it shoots up. It'd be about prediction, timing, and aim.

With multiplayer, one player would be the crocodile, the other the bug.
