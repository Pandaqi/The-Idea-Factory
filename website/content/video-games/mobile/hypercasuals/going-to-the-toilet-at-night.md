A very silly game about a very recognizable occurrence in daily life.

You get a random house / path / situation (side view).

You must simply travel to the toilet, flush, and get back to bed. Without stumbling, making noise, waking others, hurting yourself.

*Similar aesthetic/idea as the "We Steal Gravity". Maybe we can combine them?*

The game is obviously most fun if your movement is very wobbly. You're sleep drunk. So your limbs randomly move, and only when *you* press a button, do they shoot forward/freeze/snap to something. At which point a different limb starts moving.

This keeps it one button, lighthearted, physics fun.
