You're some blob, anchored to the center of the screen with an elastic. 

* Hold + move to pull yourself outward. 
* Release to let go, shooting with realistic elastic forces.

What's the point? You can use this to control things coming at you, send them somewhere. Maybe there's a "goal" or "target" or "treasure" in the center, and you're trying to protect it.
