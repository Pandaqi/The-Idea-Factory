You are a _spring_ trying to move around, reach places, dodge enemies.

* Tap/Hold to compress your spring. 
* Tap again to unload (and shoot away with force in that direction, like a real spring)

Feels like a cool, physics-based, simple mechanic. Not sure how to turn it into a game.

You'd probably have to shoot your wobbly, flibbery character to other places on the map. Like a platformer or scroller.
