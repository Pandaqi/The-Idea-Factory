Simplified soccer where players are a battery. (Just an upright 3D cylinder, perhaps with arms/legs/face.)

* Recharge your energy by picking up stuff around the field.
* Press a button to use your charge and go supersonic.

Your current energy level is displayed inside you. (With thick disks stacking on top each other, like the usual battery icon.)

Normally, moving is very *slow*, but also doesn't *drain/require energy*.

But you can press a button!

-   This gives you **superspeed**
-   And activates any powerup you have at the moment
-   But quickly **drains energy** => once depleted, the powerup stops, and you can't move at all.

## Objective

Score more goals than the opponent in the given time.

## Input

Moving + One Button

## Game Rules

### Supersonice

**Rule #1: Supersonic.** Hold your button to *use* your charge and go supersonic. (You move faster, hit the ball faster, etcetera.)

The more energy you have, the more pronounced this effect is, and the longer you can stay in it of course.

When going fast, you can bump others. When going top speed, walking into them destroys them.

### Charging

**Rule #2: Charging.** Going supersonic depletes your charge. Each map has different ways to recharge yourself: pick up something, stand somewhere, etcetera.

However, if you *overcharge* (more than 120% for example), you explode.

### Exploding

**Rule #3: Exploding.** When you explode, your corpse stays behind where you are. It becomes a "special attraction" for some time. (For example, it attracts the ball when it comes near.)

You respawn at fixed spawn points after a few seconds.

### Map

**Rule #4: All other special elements go through the *map*.** Parts of the floor might have a specific terrain that does something to you (when walking over it). Goals are different. Shapes are different. There might be other obstacles. Other ways to recharge. Other outlets for your energy.

But there are *no* other buttons, *no* powerups, *no* other systems to explain or use.

### Referee

**Rule #5: Referee.** If you bump into the referee, it's a foul. Other bumps/explosions/events might also be fouls, but that depends on the *energy level* of the referee.

A foul means your energy is drained? Your whole team is drained? The other team is completely filled? A set piece?

### Multiple Batteries?

**Rule #6 (Idea): Each player has multiple characters**. (Maybe only on low player counts.) Pressing another button shuts one off and activates the other. The one that's shut off automatically recharges.

(Switching might also be done using a different method: standing somewhere, hitting each other, whatever.)
