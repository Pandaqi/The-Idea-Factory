## What's the idea?

Simplified soccer, but with everything exactly *opposite* from what you'd expect.

I think it's best to incorporate this via **big modifiers:** each game, one or two of these are shown which will change a major rule for the rest of the game.

Ideas:

-   The slower the ball goes in, the more points you get for the goal.
-   Goals only count if the opponent touches the ball last.
-   If the ball hits you, *you* fly away (instead of the ball)
-   The team with the *least* points wins this round!

Maybe all *movement* is in reverse by default?

And the button does something *all the time*, unless when you *press/hold it*.

**Or we can make this even more general:** apply some of these rules for the whole game.

-   You want to score the *least* points
-   The ball is automatically attracted to the closest goal, so you're basically trying to protect the *opponent's goal*.
-   But ... this would just mean the same game as soccer, but you're being confused about colors. How to actually make this work?

**Could it be that teams have a special power? Team A has a power, team B has the power *opposite* to that?**

-   Example: team A walks *super fast* (so fast it's hard to control), team B walks *super slowly*
-   Example: when team A hits the ball soft, it goes absolutely flying. When team B hits the ball hard, it only moves softly.
-   Example: team A can only hit balls with curve, team B can only hit straight shots
-   Example: team A can only move vertically, team B only horizontally. (The other directions are for jumping/something else?)

And these might then switch every round, or even during rounds, as well.

**GREAT IDEA:** You don't try to get the ball into the goal ... you try to get the *goal* around the *ball*. (So you really have limited influence on the ball itself. But you can push and pull the goals around.)

### Input

Movement + One Button

### Objective

Score more points than the other team(s)
