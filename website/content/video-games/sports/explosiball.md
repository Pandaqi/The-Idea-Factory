You are characters running over a field. By default, there is nothing to actually kick or score with.

But you can **tackle** each other. Doing so **explodes** the other and splits them into *parts* ("scrap").

> **Score points by putting those parts into other goals.**

Why would this work?

-   You could assign roles in teams (one that mostly tackles, one that mostly scores)
-   The number of parts, type of parts, shape of parts, it can all be somewhat *random*
-   You only need **one** action: tackle. (Which acts as a dash for scoring the parts as well.)
-   You could give different parts other varied properties.
-   It is never too easy, because you MUST do at least one successful tackle before you even have something to kick around and score points with.

Alternative name: _Junkball_.
