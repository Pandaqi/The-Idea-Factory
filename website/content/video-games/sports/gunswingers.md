All players are **simple balls/spheres** rolling around **holding "guns"**. You're playing a simple ball sport where you try to get the ball into the opponent's goal.

* If the ball hits you on the gun side, it's automatically **put into the gun**. 
  * Pressing your button will then shoot the ball.
* If not, the ball just bounces off of you normally, following physics.

How to prevent ball hogging? 

* You automatically shoot after X time / you become slower when holding the ball.
* There's another type of ball that you can pick up and shoot, which acts like "tackling" => if it hits a player, you blast them away and they lose the ball.

Could this be a One Button Game?

* You can only _move_.
* If you ever stop moving, and your gun has a ball, you instantly shoot.
* Otherwise, it just shoots after X seconds.
