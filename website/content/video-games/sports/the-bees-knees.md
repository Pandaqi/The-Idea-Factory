Simple, online game about a made up sport played by Bees.

**IDEA 1:** You control multiple bees at the same time. Every bee is stuck in one spot (horizontally), and can only be moved up and down (vertically). Your 5 bees are scattered across the field. In moving them up and down you must defend yourself, and score in the opponent's goal. (Like *foosball* = table soccer.)

**IDEA 2:** Fifa-like game, but in small. You automatically control the bee closest to the ball, though you can switch if you want. You can move up/down/left/right and have some special shots (backflip, sliding, etc.). The ultimate goal is, again, to get the bal in the opponent's goal, and keep it out of your own.

**IDEA 3:** Something more focused on bees in particular, and what they do. (Fly, sit on plants, transfer stuff from one plant to the next.)

Something somewhat resembling _Quidditch_. So, for example, two different things to keep track of. Different parts or ways to score. Different "types of players" on the pitch.

_Alternative name: Bumblebottom Brawlers_
