The computer simulates some sports game. Potentially a completely made-up game with randomized rules. 

> _You_ play the **referee**!

As you watch the game unfold, you try to figure out what the actual rules are. What you should punish and what not. When you should interfere or not. 

Try to get a good enough score when this match ends.
