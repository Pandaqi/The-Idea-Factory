In just a few words: **Local multiplayer cooperative golf.**

Everyone chooses their angle/direction _simultaneously_. Everyone has X seconds to pick their new direction. Once the timer runs out, or everyone has *confirmed* their action, you shoot (also _at the same time_).

## Controls

**Keyboard =** LEFT/RIGHT to aim shot. UP to power up/down.

**Controller** = JOYSTICK to aim. ANY BUTTON to power up/down.

When power button is released, it is final and confirmed.

## Why a good idea?

Minigolf is a *very easy and intuitive* thing people are familiar with.

But most don't allow multiplayer. Or you're taking turns.

In this case, you all play *simultaneously*, *all the time*. There's no downtime. Things happen automatically at good speed.

Courses should be created to have as many moments to help/hinder each other as possible.
