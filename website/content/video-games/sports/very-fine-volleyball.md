Volleyball, but there's a rotating roster of "team-based rules". Example: 

* "Only one player can be on/off the floor at a time"
* "A _different_ player from your team needs to hit the ball each time."
* "You're connected to another player on a rope; you influence each other."
* "The player who last hit the ball ... must _dodge_ the ball the next time it comes into your half."
* Etc.
