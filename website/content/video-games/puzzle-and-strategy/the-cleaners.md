A puzzle game for those who get a lot of satisfaction from cleaning something.

## Objective

Clean the whole field.

## Input/Action

Tap a certain *row* or *column*. **A broom will slide across it and clean the whole row/column.**

You can tap those from both sides. This can make a difference! Because something might **block or deflect the broom halfway**.

> For example, each row has an arrow to the *left* and to the *right*. Tap one of them to execute the action.

## Map

The field starts with lots of dirty spots. You can do something to clean them (swipe, draw a line, tap to activate some machine, slide a broom across).

Of course, there are different *brooms*, and there are things in the field that *stop them*. (Actual solid walls, special teleporters or deflectors, etc.)

> These brooms are shown at the start, in sequence. You don't pick one yourself, as it'd require extra UI and buttons.
