An educational puzzle game idea to teach _directions/2D geometry_, themed around Easter. I ended up deciding to spend less time on "themed" content (Easter, Christmas, etc) and therefore discarded the idea, but it should work fine.

There are carrots in a grid. Every carrot points in some direction (like an arrow). 

You control a bunny hopping around. 

* They can go as far forward/backward as you want in the **direction they currently face**.
* They will **rotate to match** the direction of the last carrot they ate.

Later Difficulties = some more fun rules about hopping, other types of food, special squares, etc.
