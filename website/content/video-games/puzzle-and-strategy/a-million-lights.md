3D puzzle (platformer?). 

Every level puts you in a village (or just new fantasy world setting) **in the dark**. Your job is to **light up the place**.

I'm thinking of these pretty visuals with Christmas lights, cozy fires, etcetera.

**Go through the different time periods:**

* Start with fire + firestones
* Then candles
* Then glow bulbs ( + electricity mechanic)
* Then LEDS and stuff
* Then solar energy, wind energy, neon lights, etc.

The game **Bonfire Peaks** has a nice style and somewhat similar idea, use as inspiration?

Alternative Title: **And then there was light**
