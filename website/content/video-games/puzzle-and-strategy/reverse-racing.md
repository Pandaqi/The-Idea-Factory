A RACING puzzle with a twist. 

Just a grid with several horizontal lanes. Finish line at the end (right).

Some cars in spaces. 

> Each car wants to **finish at a specific rank** (1/2/3/4)

Each turn, tap a car to move it forward.

* If that would make it drive into other cars, it moves sideways.
* If that also isn't possible, it moves backward?
* If completely stuck, you just can't move it.

Now try to make them all finish in the right way!
