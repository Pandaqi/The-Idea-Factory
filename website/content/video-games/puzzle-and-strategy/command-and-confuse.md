The idea is simple: a **4X game** (like _Age of Empires_), but simplified and made for **local multiplayer**.

-   **Explore:** a random world is generated; you start on a small bit of it and need to find the rest
-   **Expand:** you slowly build more structures, create a bigger settlement, settle at other locations
-   **Exploit:** you "use" the resources and your entities to build, invent, update, create, etcetera
-   **Exterminate:** to win, you need to conquer or completely kill all the other players

So it ...

-   Can be played with multiple people behind the same screen
-   Can be played using just a few keyboard buttons, or one controller per player
-   Is more family friendly and chaotic.
-   Accessible and quick, yet the strategic and growth element of 4X remains.

How to achieve this?

-   Everything goes through *commands* or *commanders*. At all times, you control *one* thing in your clan. You can *position it* and *choose a command to execute*, that's it.
    -   Commanders issue their command to all entities inside their radius.
    -   (Or all entities "attached" to them, if I make that a thing.)
-   This only works if the number of entities is few, and the commanders all do *different* (unique) things.
    -   For example, one commander can only *build a barracks* or *collect wood*.
    -   So if you want more people to collect wood, you walk up to a bunch of people *not* doing that, press the command button, and voila.
    -   When building: they'll build it on your current location
    -   When collecting: they'll just go to their nearest source
-   Many aspects should be *automated*. For instance, you might automatically *cycle* through all your "commanders" or "entities" on a timer.

Obviously, with these different commanders, it's also great to have different *factions/clans* with very unique commands and playstyles.

**IDEA:** Some commands shorten/increase the timer for the cycle. (For example: you insta-switch to the next commander in line.)

**IDEA:** Commands might be more (semi-)random, specific, and then part of a longer list. So it might be something like:

-   "Turn two people into bowmen"
-   "Create a barrack and a house"
-   "Immediately destroy all trees in radius"

These are then positioned in a long vertical bar, and the longer you hold the button, the more it moves towards later options.

So what are the controls?

-   Joystick/Arrow keys to move
-   Button: short press = command 1
-   Button: long press = command 2 (or double press?)

How to deal with the 4X's?

-   **Explore:** A fog is on the screen. Obviously, once *somebody* enters a space, it's revealed. But the fog also returns if nobody has been there for long enough. (This still gives the explorer the advantage
-   **Expand:** Being a single screen, space is limited. As such, expansion should either be *slow*, or *very quick and very temporary*.
-   **Exploit:** Many resources are in this world. The basic ones are very easy to access, the harder ones take a lot of time (to balance it). Additionally, though, there's a huge emphasis on *regrowth* and *renewable resources*.
    -   (Maybe even your population just dies of old age, so you need to constantly keep that up as well.)
-   **Exterminate:** It's boring to be "out of the game" or wiped out so completely you are *basically* out. So, instead, there should be multiple ways to win, so you always have a shot.
    -   Simpler alternative: the game just ends once *one* player is defeated; the one with the highest score wins.
    -   Simpler alternative: each player has some special object, once captured, they are now *your ally*. (However, you can only capture it if the other person is weak enough, or there's a severe penalty to becoming an ally this way. Otherwise people can just gang up at the start of the game.)

A good name might be _Creative Commanders_ or _Command & Confuse_.
