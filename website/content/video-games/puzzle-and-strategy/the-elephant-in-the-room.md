## Actual Practical Idea

You're an elephant that can move left/right/up/down.

You're inside a *room*. The level has random walls. Your objective is to *get out*. (Probably an exit square at the edge, but might be any square.)

The perspective is *almost* top-down, with pretty low walls, so vision is never obscured (even in flat 2D).

Your elephant can be in **one of three states:** small, regular, big.

The rules are as follows.

-   Small = can pass through doors/small spaces, can share a cell with another thing/elephant
-   Regular = nothing special
-   Big = cells will break down after you stand on them, can use weight triggers / triggers "high on the wall", can *break* walls by moving through them
    -   There are two main walls? One like a door for which you need to be *small*, one like a box or bench which you can step over if you're *big*.

Different tiles will grow/shrink you when you step on them. (Speech bubbles? Food?) Maybe a more **thematic/interesting** approach is to introduce other elephants/animals/people inside the room.

-   If you're *on* or *next to* somebody (with no wall in-between), you shrink.
-   Otherwise, you grow.

Is this a "meter" and it takes several steps to switch from small\<-\>large? Feels a bit too mathematical/detailed.
