An educational puzzle game idea to teach _shapes_ (and logical thinking / problem solving, like all puzzles). The name is a pun on "Space Station". 

The screen shows a simple path/constellation of **points and lines between them**. Like a polygon, with all vertices large circles.

* You are a vehicle/spaceship/whatever that **moves from vertex to vertex**.
* On your "turn", you must **tap** one vertex (which can have all sorts of consequences) --> then the spaceship auto-moves again.
  * Perhaps some vertices are "superspeed" and will move you more, some will freeze you, etc we can play with that too.

Your goal is simply to **complete the entire path** (no obstructions, no blockage, no endless moves needed). Visit every vertex of the shape, get back home = where you started.

In its simplest mode, tapping a vertex might just _remove_ the content or _weaken_ the monster inside. (And then some monsters/moons/whatever can ONLY be removed when the game is in a certain state. Like, maybe it alternates between DAY and NIGHT.)

This could probably work ... but it's not _really_ a puzzle unless you add a few more rules (like the one above), at which point it becomes too complicated for the target audience.
