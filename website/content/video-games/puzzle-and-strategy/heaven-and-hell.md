Another 2-player, cooperative, puzzle game.

I have this vision of two worlds on the screen, reversed (so it's hard to look at the screen of the other player).

* The world at the top is **Heaven**
* The world at the bottom is **Hell**

So one player controls Heaven, one player Hell. The objective would be to get the right people in the right place, of course.

Stuff you do will have effects on what happens at the other side. Characters can pop up either way. 

Maybe some people are "not decided yet", and you actually need to _make_ them angelic or evil, so you can get them to the right place?
