A puzzle (or maybe a board/card game) where everything has two components: **what it does** and **in which direction**.

For example, you might have a "move a monster"-spell. But you can't just apply it willy-nilly. You need a second card to tell you _in which direction_ that spell is cast. If you want to remove the monster to your right, you need to plan ahead and make sure you get the "apply horizontal" card too.
