A puzzle game about collecting limbs and heads.

**The Gimmick?** Each "character" is nothing more than a torso. Their other **body pieces must be collected**, which then gives them a new power:

- **Legs:** ability to move
- **Arms:** ability to throw stuff ( / carry / break / whatever)
- **Head:** ability to see stuff

This should allow creating cool puzzles. Ones where you have pick up the right body pieces to be able to use them later for their specific power. 

Also give a reason to _get rid of them too_. Such as not fitting through gaps if you have limbs attached.
