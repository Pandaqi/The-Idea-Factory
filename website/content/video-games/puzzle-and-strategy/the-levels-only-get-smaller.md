A puzzle game (or maybe even platformer?) where levels only get *smaller and smaller*.

* So the very first level is huge ... yet very simple and obvious to solve.

Some type of "collapsing" or "imploding" gameplay? Something about volumes/space?

Maybe about **packing/unpacking =>** there is stuff, and there are boxes. Get the stuff in the boxes. You can do that by either making the boxes bigger, or the stuff smaller.
