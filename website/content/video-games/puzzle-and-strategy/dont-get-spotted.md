A fairly simple/standard colony/city building game: Start from nothing, chop some trees, etcetera.

> Except everything you do makes you MORE NOTICEABLE!

You emit more light, emit more noise, take up more space, higher chance of running across scouts, etcetera. (Could be a space theme with Dark Forest vibes.) 

And so you have to balance trying to expand and grab resources ... with being spotted and dying a terrible death. 

----> This is a way to completely remove "intelligent enemies" from the equation (which are always hard to code). Now it's just you against some metric/number/map of "being spotted or not"
