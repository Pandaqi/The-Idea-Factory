An "escape the zoo" puzzle with many animal characters.

## Level

A section of the zoo, maybe even only a single enclosure.

## Goal

There are several animals. Each of them must escape their cage/enclosure

## Input

You can "use" each of the characters, but they can all only do *very* specific things. For example:

-   One can only jump two squares in the direction they're facing
-   One can only move if they must *jump* over something (like a fence)
-   One can only break fences/doors/objects, must be carried by another animal
-   One can "scare away" other people (and animals?)

To make this work, all characters would need cool and clear *animations*. Don't know if I can make that myself right now.
