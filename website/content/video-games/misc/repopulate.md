A game where you start with **exactly one animal**. You get to choose the _type_ at the start, as each type has slightly different properties and gameplay. You could be a bunny, a cat, a bee, a fox, whatever in a random environment.

Now your goal is to **grow your population**. Keep finding food, keep getting kids, keep defending against threats, until your population has reached X size and you've beaten this environment.

In a sense, this is just the "big number go up"-genre distilled to its essence and given an intuitive (and hopefully cute/cozy) animal theme.

_Similar Idea: An infinitely upscaling kingdom builder or city builder (like that Hexagod game I saw). Every time you only work on one section, then once you call it "DONE", it will keep doing what it does forever and you move to a new one._
