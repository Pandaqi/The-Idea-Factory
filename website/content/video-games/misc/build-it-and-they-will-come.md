# (Build It) And They Will Come

Desktop/offline/hands-off idle. 

**Use the idea of "zoning" from SimCity/Cities Skylines in such a game!** 

> All you do is _mark squares as specific zones_ (if you can pay that). Over time, automatically, _villagers (cute blobs? animals?) will arrive_ and build their home and stuff. 

This is the DEFINITION of "do one thing, then sit back and return to see thing have progressed".

You can obviously upgrade how many zones you can buy, the quality of them, the types, the overall area/plot you possess, etcetera.

**@IDEA:**

* At the start of the game, you can choose the "game clock". Example: 1 day in-game = 6 hours in real life; 1 day in-game = 12 hours in real life; etc.
* Every in-game **day**, the "things that will come" refresh. Example: today Donut Shops will come to commercial zones. The next day, Hairdressers come. And so forth. (Some days there might be multiple things per zone to add some randomness.)
* In this way, you still get **control** over what exactly arrives. And you can use that to actually get **gameplay**: optimize the city by getting the right things next to each other/near each other/NOT next to each other.
