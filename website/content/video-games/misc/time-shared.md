A calm, relaxing, more philosophical video game. You start out with a large supply of "time". Your character is young and energetic and has their life ahead of them.

As you traverse the game world, you meet all sorts of challenges and characters. Most challenges can be solved ... by giving some time from _yourself_ to the right things/people.

You must cooperate and share regularly. This is necessary to win the game and solve certain events or (emotional) situations. But the game is also about being wise with _whom_ you give your precious time ...
