Basically, **POKEMON + DECK BUILDER**. 

You fight battles using your deck (or it's a peaceful strategy building game). 

* When you capture a monster, it becomes a _card_ for you. 
* HOW you capture that monster---in what exact state, using what type of card, etcetera---determines **the exact CARD it becomes**. 
* (And there's some slight randomness here that should make you excited to see what your capture ended up becoming.)

Why do I love this idea?

* This sidesteps the really annoying repetitive move-switch system from Pokemon.
* It's a really unique take that makes every part important.
* It starts making sense to cull your deck, or apply stuff to upgrade _cards_, etcetera.
