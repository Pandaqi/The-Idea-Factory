Desktop/offline/hands-off idle.

Basically, it generates this **massive map**. From time to time, you have enough power/money/strength to ...

> **Click the (grid-based) terrain to dig**

Obviously you can upgrade the _size_ of the rectangle with which you click, the _power_ it applies to each square, etcetera.

Over time, you'll discover bones, gold, secrets, etcetera that lead to currency and **hints about where the treasure is**. (These hints don't need to be great. They can just be "near a palm tree", and you see there are 5 possible spots, and you can just _try the next one each day_.)

Until, after a while, you should be able to know where the treasure is AND be strong enough to dig that deep and find it.

Which is how you beat the game :)



This _could_ be combined with actually managing a ship and sailing from island to island, and shooting up pirates. It could be a "test" for my bigger game.

* The pirates will ignore each other OR fight on their own ...
* ... but you can ACTIVELY aim your cannons and play to increase the chances of winning any encounter

(More of an auto-battler then in that regard.)
