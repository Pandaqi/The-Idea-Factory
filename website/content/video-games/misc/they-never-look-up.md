A **horror game** about doing stuff in a spider web infested dark forest.

You have to look ahead of you to see where you're going, what to pick up, etcetera. 

But above you ... **spiders are crawling and they might come down**. 

Different spiders have different tells. Some cast shadows, some don't. Some make sounds, some don't. Etc.

The capsule image can just be this stark black and white thing with a spider falling down from a single thread or something.
