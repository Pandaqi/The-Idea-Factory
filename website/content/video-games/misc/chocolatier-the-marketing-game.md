Working title is **CHOCOLATIER** (or **Sell Me This Pen**)

Roguelite/Incremental/Small Management game about running your own chocolate shop or something.

You have this tiny area of the city around you which you can access/deliver to, and a small bit of money and resources.

There are several systems you're juggling ...
* "MARKET RESEARCH": Send people into the city to _reveal_ what kinds of chocolate products people already want.
* "MARKETING": Place billboards strategically to _make_ people want specific chocolate products (or ingredients)
* "MAKING": Actually _buy_ the resources you need and decide what to _turn them into_.
  * "INVENTING" (probably a subsystem of the other): Actually inventing new products, upgrading your technology, upgrading your vehicles, etcetera)

The core of this idea that made me excited was really the **MARKETING**. This game is _not_ about making the best products, or getting the best ingredients, none of that. It's about _marketing_ it better than your competitors. 

* The strategical placement of billboards, pamphlet distribution, radio broadcast, whatever ...
* To _make_ as many people as possible want the thing you can sell the best right now.

Don't think anything like it really exists yet in video games. (Whereas several board games already used these kinds of mechanics to get really great, competitive, interactive gameplay.)
