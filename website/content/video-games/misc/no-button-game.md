This is just a joke video game idea I once wanted to submit to a game jam, but didn't.

It just starts, checks the time, and tells you if you've won. It might also tell the *next* time period you can win. (Does it check your wins?)

So opening = the only interaction.

Might post this on itch.io with a sarcastic bit about ...

> The past decennium saw games becoming *simpler* and *simpler*. Everything is so easy *anyone can instantly play*. Everything is *one button*.
>
> Well, lemme tell ya, I have something better: a **no button game**.
>
> It's obviously the next step in this revolution. *This* is what games were meant to be. We are *finally* at the *pinnacle* of game design.
>
> Start the game. It tells you if you've won or lost. Repeat.
>
> I'm eagerly awaiting my "Game of the Year" award. A Nobel Prize would do, too.

(All jokes aside, I was trying to come up with the absolute simplest game I could imagine. This is what came out of that brainstorm session. Let's pretend this is some deep philosophical statement against making games too simple, or something like that.)
