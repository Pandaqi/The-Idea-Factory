You're trying to **get the apple to fall on Newton's head.** 

You have a tree full of apples. 

Tap one to make it come loose. 

Some are worth more points, or do different things, or whatever.

- Obviously, Newton moves around.
- And there are other people who do *other* things when an apple hits them. (Which you might want, or *not* want.)
- To keep going longer, you'll have to *regrow* apples as the game goes on and time ticks by. (Apples that haven't been used for a while will *rot*, or *fall down by themselves*.)
- Ad opportunities: extra life to continue, different apple types to unlock, different arenas/people to unlock
