**A game about indirectly guiding someone else.**

The point is not to move your own character directly, but help guide and nudge some other character in the right direction.

On single player, you guide a computer-controlled character. (The **princess.** But that's mostly meant as a metaphor, it doesn't need to be a *literal* princess.)

On multiplayer, players are paired up. (Though still in two teams? So there's some cooperation, instead of just playing alongside each other without interacting?)

You can **move.** You pick up anything you walk over. If you were already holding something, you drop it.

Most things in the world automatically *move* or *change*. This way you can *time* when you swap objects to get the full effect.

- Certain objects make the princess walk towards it.
- Others make the princess walk in the opposite direction.
- Some will make her stop walking, others will make her walk faster.

**Objective?** Simply get the princess at some target destination. This is marked clearly with arrows, a flag, shining light, etcetera. It's on the same screen. It should feel like "huh I can get the princess there easily" ... and then it's not so easy.

_Don't want any connotations with sex workers though. Rename to something like "The Unwanted Guide" or "The Unwanted Bodyguard" or "The Unwanted Envoy"_
