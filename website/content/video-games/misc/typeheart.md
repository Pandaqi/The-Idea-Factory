Everything is done through *key hints* / *prompts* / *tutorial*.

At any moment, different actions appear on the screen, with the *keys* needed to execute them underneath.

All you do is **look at the options and pick the best ones.**

So, I still need the specifics of your objective and gameplay. But this general mechanic of "actions appear + unique button to do them if you want" has potential.

**Touchscreen?** These prompts are simply clickable buttons on the screen. (Like my sister's idea about just presenting options and clicking one.)

**Gamepad?** The same idea. But now the prompts will also designate *who* must press it => just a player number. (And the prompts aren't keys, but just symbols+colors.)

**Multiplayer?** That's automatically covered. (With more players, you can simply add way more prompts, so you can't even do it all with your own hands.)

Related idea: a game to learn touch typing, mostly for kids.
