A **horror game** about **navigating tall grass** (either from within or above) with who-knows-what lurking around ready to attack you. A predator animal, a monster, a swarm of insects, whatever. 

Also, you find hints along the way of what it is, where it's been, etcetera.

Instead of animals, it could just be **scary stone totems or statues** (or whatever 3D assets I can get for free/make myself). Need no animations. Easier to produce.
