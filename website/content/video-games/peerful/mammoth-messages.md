This is a _Peerful_ game. Everyone connects with their phone to a main screen, and plays on their own phone.

A modified version of _Drawful_, using vague caveman drawings.

**Step 1:** All players draw 3---5 random symbols on their phone. Freeform drawing, whatever you like. (Perhaps prompted with simple words.)

**Step 2:** Then all players get a random word + a set of those caveman symbols to pick from. They must pick 3 or 4 caveman drawings to **communicate that word**.

**Step 3:**

* Each drawing is shown one by one, and players input their _own_ guess as to what the caveman symbols stand for.
* Then all answers are combined with the true one, and players must vote on the one they think is correct.
* Then the answer is revealed and scored.
