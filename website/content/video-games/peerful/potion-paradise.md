This is a _Peerful_ game. Everyone connects with their phone to a main screen, and plays on their own phone.

On the main screen, multiple recipes are being worked on simultaneously.

Your phone has several buttons (fields / areas), representing one big button _per recipe_.

When you click that button, you add the ingredient you currently hold to the recipe immediately.

The gist of the game is thus ...

* You constantly have to check (or remind each other) if you're needed somewhere (and by exactly how much).
* Then tap the button that corresponds with that location.

To make it more challenging, we might add ...

* BUYING: You also need to restock your ingredients. (Maybe you control _multiple_ ingredients and must properly switch between them. Which might also take time.)
