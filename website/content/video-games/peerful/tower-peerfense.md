This is a _Peerful_ game. Everyone connects with their phone to a main screen, and plays on their own phone.

A Tower Defense game might be uniquely suited to this kind of game.

All players control **different parts of the tower**. 

* One player can only rotate weapons. As such, their interface only shows each weapon + rotate button behind it.
* Another player can only power up the weapons. Again, they get a unique interface on their personal phone. them up,
* One player explores the area
* One player deploys armies
* Etcetera

Waves of monsters come in from all sides. Defend!
