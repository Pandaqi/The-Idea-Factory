This is a _Peerful_ game. Everyone connects with their phone to a main screen, and plays on their own phone. (Like the _Jackbox_ games.)

Some of the classic games seem a good fit for this style of game.

* **Snake:** directions for movement scattered between players. (Perhaps multiple snakes, if many devices.)
* **Pacman:** Some people are Pacman, some are Ghosts. There are some special abilities that pop up; a button on your phone.
* **Pong**: speaks for itself. You move your own paddle. If many players, multiple people control the same paddle. Protect your own goal. => This is more like a **top-down soccer** that way, which would be a logical next step.

Any classics I'm missing or that might fit better?
