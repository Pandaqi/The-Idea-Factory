This is a _Peerful_ game. Everyone connects with their phone to a main screen, and plays on their own phone.

The main screen keeps giving you all sorts of **instructions**. (Stand on one leg, find an object, shake hands with every other player, etcetera.)

You have to follow them, either in real life or through some minigame on your phone.

* This can be a hidden role game (like Suspicious Fishes) => you give yourself away if you execute the prompt too late or the wrong way.
* Or it can just be a skill game => you don't get points / are eliminated if you fail too often.
