This is a _Peerful_ game. Everyone connects with their phone to a main screen, and plays on their own phone.

This game is pretty much the **Prisoner's Dilemma**, repeated.

* The screen constantly shows battles / dilemmas between players. (Example: If both cooperate, both get 5 money. If one cooperates and one defects, the latter gets 10 money. Etcetera.)
* On their phone, they only get two options: cooperate or defect.

Once both have chosen (or the timer has run out), the results are shown and handled. 

Repeat.
