A music quiz game, where you play the music yourself. A _Peerful_ game: all players play on their own phone, connected to a shared computer/game.

One player gets a list of options for songs. They should be very popular and well-known. They pick **one** they want to execute.

The players are divided into two groups: musicians and guessers.

* The **musicians** receive the song name _and_ a random instrument. **They must play the song, together.** Tapping their screen to activate/play their instrument, adding to the overall song being played.
* The **guessers** must **correctly guess it**, based on that.

Need to figure out the details about points, whether guesses are sent to the computer (or what the authority does anyway), etcetera. But the core should be really strong.

Those random instruments are mostly silly ways to make sounds.

* One half of a piano octave.
* Part of a drum kit
* Buttons that create specific sounds
* Strings (guitar, violin, etcetera) that sound when you swipe across them.
* Random funny sound effects
