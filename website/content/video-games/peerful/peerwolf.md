This is a _Peerful_ game. Everyone connects with their phone to a main screen, and plays on their own phone.

This system should work _great_ for **hidden role or deception games**, like Werewolf.

* People get a secret role on their phones.
* This changes the literal things they can do. The prompts they get. The controls they have / their interface. Etcetera.

Put it all together, try to achieve something, then vote for the traitor afterwards.
