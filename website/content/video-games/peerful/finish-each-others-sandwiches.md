You repeatedly get prompts on your phone. (This is a _Peerful_ game. Everyone connects with their phone to a main screen, and plays on their own phone.)

But you can only see the **previous word** or **_part_ of the sentence** you're building.

At the same time, on the _main screen_, the sentences are connecting and building a story.

The gist of the game is thus ...

* Constantly check your screen _and_ the main screen for clues where the sentences/story are going.
* Then try to find a fitting (but funny/useful) next word to put into the blanks left for you.

Rinse and repeat until all sentences are done and the final story is displayed on-screen.
