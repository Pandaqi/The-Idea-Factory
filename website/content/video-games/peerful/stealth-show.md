With my "Peerful Project", I discovered that you can easily connect all player's phones to the same computer screen. Then I realized this setup is ideal for **stealth games**. Because everyone plays (partially) on their own phone, the game can easily hide where people are or what they are doing. But over time, information and a map reveal themselves on that central screen.

## Idea 1

Realtime game played on smartphones (or with two computers??)

One person (or a team of people?) is trying to perform a mission without being seen/detected/caught.

The rest are guards trying to prevent this from happening.

(We *can* use a computer as a sort of central hub, but it would be worthless to make it a map for everyone, as that would remove the stealth aspect. Although, we could make it so that only *one* player is playing from his phone, the rest plays from the computer.)


## Idea 2

A sound-based game. One person is the spy/stealth person who actually sees the computer and tries to perform a mission without being seen.

All other people cannot see the screen but must rely on sound only. (Different volumes of sounds, different distribution across Left/Right, different *types* of sounds at different moments, etc.)
