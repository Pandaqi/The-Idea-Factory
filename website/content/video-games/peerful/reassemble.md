This is a _Peerful_ game. Everyone connects with their phone to a main screen, and plays on their own phone.

**You have an area to sort / clean up / get back together.** 

Maybe a shop, puzzle blocks, whatever. The pieces are scattered around the entire map.

Players simply have to run around, grab the right pieces, and **place them all inside the boundaries** to rebuild what was lost. The more pieces you fit within the given time, the better you score.

How to generate this?

* Simply start with the entire thing filled.
* Randomly cut/break it into pieces (of a size that matches a singular "piece" sprite)
* Then spread the pieces randomly.
* (For simpler levels, just remove a few of those pieces at random, leaving more wiggle room)
