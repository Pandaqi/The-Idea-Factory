A **local (cooperative) multiplayer game** about running a **soup restaurant**. Really, I just loved the title (_Long Time No Soup!_) and am looking for an excuse to make a game around it.

You'll have to

-   Grow the right plants yourself
-   Buy the equipment (bowls, pan, etc.)
-   Deliver/combine herbs into the equipment.
-   Deliver the soup to waiting customers just in time

However, as the screen will be occupied by all those elements, we don't really have space for customers.

So simplify it:

-   There's a timer
-   Deliver soup to reset it (delivering something better, resets it longer -- play that risk/reward game)
-   If it runs out, the level ends. ("Long Time, No Soup!")
-   Otherwise, you keep playing until you've reached the score needed to pass that level (?)
