A simple, party, local multiplayer game.

Based on that "remove the slime from the spaceship"-mechanic from GoGodot Jam 4.

-   You're all **controlling a submarine together**. The hull of that ship is covered in slime or something "bad".
-   You actually *want* certain fishes to eat damage/plants/slime from the edges of your ship. 
    -   (Can retheme that to things like "this fish plugs holes", so you want them to swim into that hole.)
-   Hitting more plants/slime along the way will stick it to wherever it hit, damaging your hull again.
-   A certain plant replenishes your oxygen tank?

In other words, you're navigating a submarine together. You actively try to avoid specific fish/plants, while actively trying to _bump/crash into_ other specific fish/plants.

A bit like _"Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime"_. But it's more focused on one mechanic that's fully explored, instead of numerous enemies/bullets/etcetera.
