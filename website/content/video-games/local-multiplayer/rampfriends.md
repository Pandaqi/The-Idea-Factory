Half the players are **balls that can roll**. Half the players are **ramps**. (Mostly just diamonds = rotated rectangles.)

The rolling players _need_ the ramp players to get higher and make jumps. So it's always a combined effort to achieve something.

This creates cooperative puzzles and platforming in a very realistic, intuitive way (as you're just following physics).

If we want, this can be a _One Button Game_.

* Rolling players can only roll one way
* With a quick-press, you can switch between "roll" and "ramp" on the fly?
