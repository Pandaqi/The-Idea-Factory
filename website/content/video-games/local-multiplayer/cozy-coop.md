I feel like **diving** or **fishing** has potential for local cozy coop (+ maybe online multiplayer). Something like [this](https://www.reddit.com/r/godot/comments/1re6a8b/custom_pixel_art_dynamic_dithering_and_caustic/) rather quickly gets a bunch of unique fish and a very interesting look. 

Something with running a "lemonade stall" kind of thing always feels cozy and contained and small. 

Or maybe fishing, diving, mining.

In general, I think a cozy incremental game could do quite well if I make that. They are "programmer" games. They don't need a lot of art or good art; just very interesting systems and numbers.

Or like a "small management" game, inspired by things like "Minami Lane" or [Boba Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1847510/Boba_Simulator__Idle_Shop_Management/)

This is another example of how effective a simple cozy game can be: <https://store.steampowered.com/app/4417620/Good_Catch/>
