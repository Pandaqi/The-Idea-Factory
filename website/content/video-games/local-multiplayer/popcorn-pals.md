You run a little popcorn stall.

You place "popcorn machines" in your little space/store.

You can drop corn into these machines, which will heat up over time, then **POP and JUMP OUT.**

Here's the simple rule: **they always fly out in the direction of your cursor**. (And then they're just accepted by whatever customer they bump into, if any.)

This creates a nice double usage of your cursor. It is _both_ used to ...

* Run the shop/resources: plant seeds, collect mais, drop it into machines, etc ... 
* As well as AIM the corn that constantly pops out on a different timer.
