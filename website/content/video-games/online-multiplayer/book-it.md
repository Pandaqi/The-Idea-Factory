A game where you have to "clean up" a space by tucking stuff into treasure chests, or drawers, or depots, or bookshelves, or whatever.

Because ... whatever is **NOT put away nicely disappears when night comes.**

And during the night you have to RETRIEVE some of the things you put away to fight monsters/complete objectives/whatever. So, you want a GOOD SYSTEM for where stuff is and how it's sorted, and good cooperation and memory, to **find the thing you wanted quickly**.

In this form, it's a cooperative online multiplayer game, somewhat cozy/relaxing (especially if you can turn off the night portion).

But it could also be a **puzzle platformer** or **action game** where _everything_ is done through books and paper.

* The idea of using MASSIVE CHUNKY BOOKS as large objects is very interesting.
* Moving platforms = books. 
* Attack = throw book. 
* Stack books = a way to get to higher places.
* Learn new skills = find the book that contains this information and read it.
* Etcetera

_Inspired by [Monk Took Book](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DQZsA2pevWg). The name "Book It" is obviously a pun on the expression that means to run away / flee / make a run for it._
