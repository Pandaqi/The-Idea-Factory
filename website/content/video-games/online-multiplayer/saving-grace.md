"Buy all the products you need while spending the least money on a busy and dangerous market."

All players get the same list of products and are dropped into the same map ( = randomly generated market/set of shops).

You're trying to **find the products**, but also the **best deals** for them. (The products are sold in many many places, so it's never frustrating to _find_ one, you just need to find the _best deal_ too.)

There are extra products you can spend money on which are merely _gadgets_. Ways to help find stuff faster, to get lower prices for things, maybe to steal something once in a while, and to prevent against dangers like pickpockets.

_Title "Saving Grace" is a pun. All players are called Grace and they try to "save money". It also applies to the moment you finally find a place that sells the last product you need for cheap; that place is your Saving Grace._
