An offline/online multiplayer **drawing game**. 

Players automatically draw lines as they move (and they're _always_ moving)

But they can **jump/slide** to get over other lines + briefly NOT draw anything themselves.

This can have many objectives ...

* **You're not allowed to touch each other.**
* **Using those lines for some sort of tower defence/shields.**
* **Recreating images/words/symbols** (perhaps that's an optional sidequest to earn a bit more points/power/shield)
* **Guessing game like pictionary** => that idea from Anticipation is _really_ clever.
  * As someone draws a thing, a dice counts down (6->5->4...)
  * If you guess it right, you move the NUMBER OF SPACES ON THAT DIE.
  * So, you need to memorize which space you want to be, and must often wait with guessing for the right moment. (Giving less strong players more of a chance + adding more strategy).
    * Higher levels also have GAPS, which means you can fall down to a lower level if you guess wrong.
    * You ascend to the next level each time you've collected each color once (Red, Green, Yellow, Blue).
