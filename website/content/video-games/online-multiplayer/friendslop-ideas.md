Somehow, simple online multiplayer games with friends have made a massive resurgence lately. And for some reason they're called "Friendslop", which is a stupid term, but I'll use it for this collection of ideas.

The typical thing is to just connect **two people to the same thing** (vehicle/rolling log/egg/etc). What else can you do with that?

* Maybe combine with _sports_? Your entire team is connected by chains or whatever, effectively moving as one person?
* Unused vehicles, like planes? Then maybe (flock/herd of) birds/animals makes more sense.
* Maybe one player _dives_, and the other has to _keep the oxygen levels appropriate_?
* Fishing together?
* Mining together?
* A game about walking dogs? About "one throws the stick, the other controls the dog and must catch it before it lands"? => One player is the dog owner, the other is the dog leashed to him?

Maybe something inspired by [this game](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1734230/WHATEVER/)?
* Either steering a boat through waters together.
* Maybe steering a hot air balloon?
* Or that one screenshot with the **alien UFO trying to abduct you** => we could make a silly fun local multiplayer game about some people being aliens, others being cows, and trying to NOT get abducted.

## Windowwashy 

Multiple players all hold a different corner of the same cloth. 

You simply have to climb up a skyscraper (2D side-view) + **move around to clean the whole thing as quickly as possible**.
* This means being efficient with movement and position.
* It also means moving around obstacles (such as something that splits the building and you both need to get to the same side)
* Perhaps even re-stitching the cloth, or growing it, or preventing birds from flying into the window and make it dirty again.

There's a "maximum stretch/area" of the cloth beyond which it will break/you can't move further. 

The cloth is just made of lots of elastically connected points in a grid, like actual soft physics does (but simplified)
