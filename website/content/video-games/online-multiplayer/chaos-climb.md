I feel like a pure side-view 2D climber, with my creative style and ideas, could still add something new. Especially as a local and online multiplayer game, everyone climbing the same mountain and fighting for handholds.

We can make it about **moving from handhold to handhold** before your** limbs/stamina/strength runs out**. (So quick decisions about which paths to take and where to go, while other players can steal handholds and get in the way.)

And then we can ALSO make it about **"placing safety ropes"** with the single interaction button you have. You must place safety anchors/ropes in the right places, at the right intervals, grabbing enough "rope-powerups" to keep yourself stocked. If you ever fall or let go, the rope (which is just a simple elasticity equation) will catch you and allow you to continue.

* You can simply move left/right freely while hanging from the rope---this barely costs any strength because it's rope-supported.
* But the moment you go BEYOND the rope again---above it, out of range---it gets loose and you're climbing on your own again.

Maybe the mountain literally becomes a _point_ near the top, forcing players to be close together more and more as you reach the top.
