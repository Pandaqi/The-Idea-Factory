Everyone starts out as the smallest fish.

* You can only eat things smaller than you.
* But the bigger you are, the _more_ food you need to stay alive.

This naturally leads to a nice risk-reward balance, where being bigger means others can't eat you ... but _you_ need to eat A LOT or you'll just starve and kill yourself.

Simply play until a single player remains alive. They win.
