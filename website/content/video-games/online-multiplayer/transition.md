Basically an incremental game, but you're **actually trying to _shrink_ instead of _grow_**.

You start with a simple map that is already randomly filled with stuff. Cars racing around, oil being dug up, factories running, people using electricity.

One by one, you need **to _transition_ to clean energy.**

* Replace cars with bikes/walking/trains.
* Find the money and space to place sustainable energy sources (solar panel, water, wind)
* So you can completely close the factories or stop the oil mining. (Though you need enough "jobs elsewhere" to be able to do that?)

You win if you keep the simulation running all the time, until you've 100% transitioned to clean sustainable stuff.
