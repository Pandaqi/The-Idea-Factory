I thought the game [Flock Around](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H5Tpn1dv3x0) was such a great idea for an online multiplayer game. 

So I wondered if we could do the same but with FISH or MAMMALS or MADE-UP MONSTERS or SOME TWIST.

* Players are dropped on this huge jungle map.
* They simply try to spot certain creatures, taking a picture of them in the right way.
  * Creatures have clear habitats and preferences and stuff. This is how you can TRACK THEM and FIND THEM. (E.g. a lion is near the carcass of another animal, a certain bird only appears in a certain climate, etc)
* But you have a limited number of pictures and light. (You can extend your speed/pictures/flashlight battery by grabbing stuff in the environment.)
* The first player to photograph them all wins!
  * Perhaps the map shrinks over time, removing opportunities to get certain animals. In that case it would just be about photograping _the most_ before time runs out completely.
