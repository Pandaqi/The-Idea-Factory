A simple online multiplayer game about **aliens trying to abduct you**.

The "aliens" could be just a "dumb" computer opponent => just a beam of light shining down from the heavens. Or it could be OTHER PLAYERS.

The **players** must **hide below buildings and trees** to make sure they are **not caught in the beam**.

You have to recharge your energy/weapons/whatever, so you can't stand still and stay in the same place. You have to make yourself vulnerable regularly to go out and grab stuff.

**Crucially, the beam lifts you up, so it is also the ONLY WAY to get to higher places.** You have to actually _use_ the alien's light beams, then make sure you get out of the area in time to drop off. (Or they'll just catch you, of course, and you lose/die.)

This is such a cool push-and-pull movement mechanic, I should really use this for something.
