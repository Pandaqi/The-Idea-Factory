A bit like hide and seek or tag, but instead of "finding/tagging" others, you're trying to pickpocket them unseen.

Everyone looks clearly different. But you **don't know who is who**. 

Some players are secretly pickpockets. 

> When you walk past someone, on a small delay (because of network, that happens anyway), they will lose money.

_This is a nice way to "incorporate" the delay/lag inherent to online games._ When you think you pickpocket someone, ask the server if you actually did. They confirm (or not) by sending back a message to both players (the pickpocketee and the one pickpocketed) updating their money.
