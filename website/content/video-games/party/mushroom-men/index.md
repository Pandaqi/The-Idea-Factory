Everyone is a **Mushroom Man.** Yes, you look like a mushroom, which doubles as a parachute as you glide down.

> **If you touch the floor, you die.** 

Stay alive as long as possible. (Or until some timer runs out / some objective is reached of the level.)

When in the air, you can throw **seeds** underneath you. When they hit something, they grow a mushroom on top of it. These act as trampolines or pillows.

**Idea:** taking turns, one player becomes the "invincible one". They *can* stand on the ground and save others by walking/jumping underneath them.

**Puns:** Fungi = "Fun guy", "Mushroom for improvement", "Crown the Champignon"
