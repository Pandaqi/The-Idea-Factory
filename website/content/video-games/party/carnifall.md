You enter a random circus arena with trampolines, trapezes, tightrope, etcetera. The game shows clear poses, tricks, and challenges. 

You must _try_ to work together and **score points for the tricks and poses**. 

> Which can be as simple as just "manage to get this high" or "be upside-down in the air".

Probably also works best if players have different _roles_. So one moves like X, another like Y, and a third is extra strong or has great balance, etcetera.
