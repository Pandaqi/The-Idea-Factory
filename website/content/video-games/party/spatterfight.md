A casual/cute "fighting game" where you win if the other player is **completely covered in paint**! (Or water, or whatever substance you're spattering.)

Inspired by swimming in pools and trying to spatter your friends as wet as possible. Or those street soccer games with water-filled bottles. Or doing the same with foam/soap, or paint, etc.

How to make?

-   Many actions result in a *particle explosion*
-   Detect if particles overlap with player. If so, *paint* their mask texture to the color of the particle.
-   Keep a low-resolution grid underneath it. If 99% filled, fill the whole body, and the player dies.

**IDEA:** You *refill* by standing in those substances. 

> Example: standing in water refills that water gun.

**IDEA:** There are objects that can be *activated/grabbed/used* by covering them as well. 

> However, these are neutral (they can be covered by anyone) and can be *good or bad*. (So you don't want to miss someone and then accidentally get yourself a terrible powerup.)

NAME: Might sound really cool in Dutch: *spettergevecht*. Or a modified version of it: *spattergevecht* or *spetterfight* (although people might mistake that for a typo).
