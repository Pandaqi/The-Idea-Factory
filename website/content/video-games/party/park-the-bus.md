A very silly party game about getting a golf ball into a hole, or a soccer ball into a goal right in front of you ...

**... but each level has a different "trick"/"sneaky way" in which it tries to make you fail.** 

Example: When you kick the ball, the goal will always move exactly 500 pixels to the left. 

Example: When you kick the ball, it will go in the opposite direction.

Every time you play, every level, you have to try stuff to figure out the sneaky rule that's making it hard for you to score. Should be really funny as the goal keeps moving or the ball keeps doing weird things, as well as satisfying when you figure out the pattern and score.

Alternative Names: YOU'RE NEVER EVER SCORING / BRICK WALL / IMMACULATE DEFENSE

_This idea should be quick to make and easy to play. I smile just thinking about the silly ways in which you can annoy players and make it hard to get the ball into the goal each level. I merely did not pursue it further because I chose to focus on multiplayer and educational games, and this one is neither._
