A shooter game, but you shoot _spaghetti_: **A physical rope object made of cylinders/spheres.** 

This means bullets are slow. But they're long and wobbly, and might hit/constrict/block people later depending on how they fall/twist around stuff. 

Maybe all that spaghetti is _poisonous_. You're not necessarily trying to hit people, but rather try to _block_ all their paths so they'll accidentally walk into your spaghetti and hurt themselves.
