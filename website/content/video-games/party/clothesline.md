You play as creatures (that look like typical clothes) ... in the sky, **moving over clothes lines**.

There are buildings (of different heights/sizes) on all sides, with clothes lines between them. You can only stick to a line and move forward/backward on it, or jump to a new line (if you aim well).

Clothes are constantly pouring in and you need to dry them all:

-   Find an empty position on the line to hang them
-   Remove them as soon as possible once dry.

Extra restrictions:

-   Some will change color if left too long in the sun
-   Maybe the sun moves/weather changes so you need to move stuff with it
-   Some clothes can't be next to others
-   Some clothes are heavy and need a stronger line => might add some special line that has a "maximum of X clothes" limit anyway

This can be a solo challenge, of course, but is perfectly suited for (local) multiplayer cooperative play.
