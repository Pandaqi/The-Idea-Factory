A **local multiplayer party game** where you **fight as a spaceship** in a **tiny randomly generated universe**.

Not sure if this should be 2D or 3D.

**Map?** A bunch of random planets in empty space.

**Goal?** Destroy all other spaceships. (Add some secondary "peaceful" goal?)

**How?** You can rotate your spaceship and thrust in your forward direction.

**What's fun/new?**

* **Gravity** attracts you towards planets all the time: use that for slingshot actions and just fun stuff
* Similarly, *gravity attracts your bullets/lasers/projectiles towards planets*
* The planets itself could have some function, some buildings, some resources. (Such as: launching from a specific planet gives you an extra boost in speed, like being fired from a cannon.)
* Different spaceships have different elements. (More guns, guns at the back, etc)

In general, this is just really *thematic* and *easy to explain/play*. (Everyone understands how a rocket works, you only rotate and thrust forward, and shoot at stuff. Also, gravity leads to interesting situations, while most people understand how that works.)

(_Spaeshbettul_ is what I imagine a toddler sounds like while trying to say *Space Battle*.)
