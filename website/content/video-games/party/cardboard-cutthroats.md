**A game with the art style of those Harry Potter LEGO game cutscenes.** (Cardboard cut-out characters bopping up and down on a multi-layered background. Like it was made from cardboard/paper and placed behind each other.)

It's probably about fighting, or stealing, or something.

**Idea 1:** You can "go down under". Your character disappears under the playing field, and you can move unseen. (By others ... but also by you.) When you press the button again, you come up wherever you are now.

**Idea 2:** When someone is knocked down, their cutout-character *folds in half* (along the waistline).
