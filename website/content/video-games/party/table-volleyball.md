For the longest time, I had this idea of **"table soccer" ... but as a volleyball game**. 

As in, the entire idea (when rotating your characters) is to **keep the ball in the air** instead of on the ground/shoot it in a goal.

In practice, this would mean:

* Each team has "many" characters (relatively speaking).
* But they're connected with a pipe, and you can only make them all rotate/move at the same time.
* So each player only has a few buttons to control one or two pipes.
* And now you must play a game of volleyball
