Incremental game. 

Each run people come in saying they've lost something. 

You get points for clicking it / finding it in a large number of things.

The more you upgrade, the bigger your box will be, which makes it harder to find the right things and help all the people coming in.

_Very similar things have already been done, and quite recently too. That's why I discarded the idea._
