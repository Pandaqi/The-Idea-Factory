**I think there's a LOT of potential for a Balatro/Slay-The-Spire thing that's way more SIMPLIFIED and EDUCATIONAL/FOR KIDS**. 

Maybe your deck is a bunch of animals and each "attempt/challenge" is placing them in a zoo. 

Maybe it's about cooking---"poker hands" are "specific recipes" instead.

* For reference: <https://www.gamespot.com/gallery/best-games-like-balatro/2900-7117/> and say <https://store.steampowered.com/app/3538860/Tabulo/>
* Yeah, "deck building" can be anything really (dominoes, dice, coins, animals, buildings, etc), could lift some board game (bag building) aspects too.

**Wait, that ZOO theme is actually brilliant?** It can become a mix between Islanders and Balatro. Certain animals obviously want to be inside certain biomes, they want to be near (or NOT NEAR) certain other animals, you can place extra amenities inside/outside the cages, etcetera.

This was the **older idea** I had years before this one, which is very similar (my brain isn't as original as I thought): 

* A Zoo Tycoon game played on a small hex grid. 
* Each turn you simply get cards / draw from your deck (like Islanders and many other games). 
* Cards can be TERRAIN (specific exhibit, or path maybe), or ANIMAL, or SOME THIRD SYSTEM. You simply click the thing to place and the hex to place it on and that's that.
