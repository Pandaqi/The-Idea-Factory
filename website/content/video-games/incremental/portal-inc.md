Alternative Name: "They Just Keep Coming!"

Your mouse controls where you **place/move portals**. Wherever they are, your troops (or ENEMY TROOPS?) **keep pouring in**. 

That's the entire game. It's like holding back a flood or building a dam. 

The flood will keep coming---you just decide WHERE and when/how long you OPEN IT. Redirect that flow, the entities pouring it, to get the best result possible.
