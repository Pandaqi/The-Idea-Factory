A simple incremental game where you **control that typical grabber machine at carnivals**. Upgrade the contents of the machine, the size of the grabber, the dependability/durability of it, etcetera to grab as much as possible each round.

Somewhat inspired by: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3424940/Prize_Denied/
