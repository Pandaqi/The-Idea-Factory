An incremental game. All you do is ...

> **You _connect_ two people on the map.** (Start at one, click+drag to other.) 
> 
> To make them fall in love + score points. 
> 
> Upgrades?

* Longer max distance
* Faster connects (the animation brings them together; you can only connect again once the previous one ended?)
* Connect MULTIPLE (so you just keep going after the first two connections)
* AUTO-CONNECT -> The people walking around will automatically connect to "their specific match" if they are within range.

PERSONAL VERDICT: This is fine, but it feels like it's missing the boat on all fronts. It's not _really_ cozy and sweet, but it's also not _really_ challenging or strategic. Both of the main target audiences will come in with the wrong expectations and be disappointed in such a game. At least that's my feeling now.
