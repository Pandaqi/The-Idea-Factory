Everything in the game is done with these **wobbly poles**. (Like the bendy ones used in athletics and stuff.)

> Click on one side to make the top _bend_ to the other side.

Alternatively, it automatically follows your mouse cursor (no clicking needed), but just oscillates around it.

You earn money by **touching/breaking stuff** with that moving pole.

Over time you can buy more poles, which bend further, which break things faster, etcetera.

The interaction mechanic should be _really fun and playful_. But I just don't see how to make a bigger game out of it that stays interesting for longer than 10 minutes.
