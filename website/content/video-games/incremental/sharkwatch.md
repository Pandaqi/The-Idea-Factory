Alternative names: "Shark Eyes" / "Sharkshooter" / "Lifeguard"

* You're running a beach. There are sharks. People are drowning.
* Click on people in the water to save them.
* Once you've saved enough people, your next click is an "exterminator" to get rid of a shark?
* The issue is that **sharks and people are hard to find**. They can go underwater.
  * You only get shadows, silhouettes, bubbles floating up if someone is (deep) underwater.
  * You can hold your mouse in some place to make whatever is below "come to the surface". (Either hovering or holding down click, not sure.)
  * Sharks go up and down. They only move horizontally or vertically because I don't feel like drawing the other sprites for that.
* With upgrades,
  * You can **click faster, in a larger radius, grabbing people from further under water.**
  * Hovering will make things **come to the surface more quickly**
  * You get a **scanner** to find sharks.
  * Your **exterminator** appears more quickly, is more powerful, in a bigger range, etcetera.
  * Maaaaybe you have a literal **boat** that has to reach people to save them? And this boat can become faster, hold more people, etcetera?

_It feels like it has potential, but now I'm treating an incremental game too much like a roguelike ..._
