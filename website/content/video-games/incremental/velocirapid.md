That idea of mine about an **incremental game** where you upgrade your **bicycle** as you perform races. 

It feels like there's something simple there: upgrade front wheel, or back wheel (make one super big and another super small to get through specific sections), stamina, burn rate, wind resistance, etcetera. 

Then we can just have a simple 2D side-view simulation of cyclists doing that race, and you're trying to get furthest.
