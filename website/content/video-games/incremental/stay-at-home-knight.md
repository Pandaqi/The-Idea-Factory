Inspired by that Magic Archery game where you merely click what quest it should go on. And then a timer starts + gives rewards when completed. (No actual quest or leaving the home happens!)

Upgrades give ...

* The ability to go out on more quest
* New _types_ of quest that can appear
* Faster resolution of quests
* Larger rewards
* (Maybe there's some element of "being caught" or "being found out", so you also need to upgrade to avoid detection, or pick quests to make sure you don't raise the suspicion meter on yourself too high.)

HAHA: Quests often show badly photoshopped photos in which your Hero/Knight pastes themselves to "prove" they really did this quest!

HAHA: Every time you go on a quest, your little knight runs out, stops, has a speech bubble that says something like "Eh. Maybe tomorrow." or "Hmm. Kinda cold today.", then turns around and goes back inside.

I think **Stay At Home Knight** is the better name. Especially because _Stay At Home Hero_ is already a book about stay at home parents and stuff.
