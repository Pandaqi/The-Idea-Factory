An incremental arcade-y game.

* Eggs appear on the screen. 
* You're **drag-throwing-flicking chickens** around to _brood_ and _hatch_ them. 
* (Once a few eggs have hatched, you move the chicken to a new place, etcetera.) 
* Upgrades bring more chickens, new eggs, faster brooding, etcetera.

Trying to hatch eggs efficiently (brood as many as possible with one chicken, moving them immediately to new spot once eggs have hatched) should provide a really clear gameplay loop and challenge. Also, love the name.
