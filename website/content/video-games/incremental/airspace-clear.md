Incremental-like game. You're an airspace controller. Drones, birds, lots of things appear and you need to **click**/shoot/whatever them all away to **prevent collisions with the airplanes**. 

Survive as long as possible. Earn points for each collision avoided.

Similar idea = "RADAR/SONAR". You can't see anything on the map. All you get is a bunch of sonars placed at random locations, sending out waves. When they hit something, it briefly shows up as a blip or outline on your screen. You must watch closely, memorize, then click exactly the right spots to gather stuff there.

_Inspired by that newspaper article about keeping drones away from Eindhoven Airport. I discarded this idea myself because it's already very similar to many existing airspace controlling casual games and I didn't see how to really make it unique._
