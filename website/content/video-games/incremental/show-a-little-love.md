Cozy idle game, barely an incremental.

You simply **hover over/click animals** to **pet them or give them a hug or some love** or whatever. 

A very cozy, very chill incremental game.

I personally discarded the idea because it would thrive _entirely_ on graphics and interactions, and those are probably my weakest skills in game dev. (Would need lots of cute animals and animations, juicy feedback, enough to distract from the fact that there's not really any gameplay. Because if we _do_ add actual gameplay and tactics, it stops being cozy and relaxing ...)

_Alternative Name: Petting Zoo._
