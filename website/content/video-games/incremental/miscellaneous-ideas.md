_A bunch of ideas for incremental games that never got far enough to receive their own file/page of notes. Too vague at the time, not better than some other idea I had, whatever the reason._

**@IDEA:** Is an incremental game about SCHOOL/LEARNING at all possible?

* Skill tree would be a literal skill tree
* Learning new stuff would allow you to _actually apply it in-game for some bonus/effect_

**@IDEA:** Is an incremental "run a shop" or "run a restaurant" game possible? In a highly simplified/abstracted manner so I only need to show basic food icons?

**@IDEA:** A plant generator/grower but from the _side_ (2D view)? This simplifies the viewpoint and options enough that it opens up more intricate (**educational**?) mechanics such as _managing soil quality_, _watering_, _providing sunlight_, _planting precisely_, etcetera in an incremental game.


**@IDEA:** Incremental golf? Incremental bowling? Incremental darts?

**@IDEA: Hacking Inc?** An incremental game about trying to hack a company. The further you get, the more points. But it will take some extra gear, and knowledge, and better computers etc to beat each level.

**@IDEA RELATED: Digital Jungle.** I just really like that saying. The idea of combining nature and hacking, the idea of "saving" a digital jungle. But what would the actual _game_ be??




**@IDEA:** "Zone Out". Some incremental/idle/cozy game that is _clearly_ meant to waste time, fill time, zone out, a little distraction. That's the whole schtick and marketing angle.

**@IDEA:** PYRAMID INC => Something about growing pyramids taller/smaller, moving them around, positioning them around the nile, crocodiles, ??
