A tiny incremental game.

The map is a 2D side-view image with random cute characters spawning at the bottom.

All sorts of dangers bubble up from below the surface / at the bottom of the screen. -> The typical thing would be lava moving upwards, but could be something else.

> **When you click while hovering over someone, they jump up.**

Upgrades allow a bigger radius (so you can make more people jump at once), jumping higher, clicking again while already in the air, etcetera.

(Because the more rewarding/powerful dangers go HIGHER, so you need to JUMP HIGHER to dodge them too.)
