A cozy incremental/idle/caretaing game about **giving your pet the best life possible**. 

Make them as large and healthy as possible, let them earn all sorts of skills, bring them to different places, etcetera. 

(Default is probably a _dog_, but I could allow picking different starting animals that completely change how the game plays.)
