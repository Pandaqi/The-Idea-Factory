A simplified incremental-like game about running a hotel _in the galaxy/on stars_. Could also be called "Ten Star Hotel" or _however many stars you need to complete to beat the game._

* After "fulfilling/beating" one star, the next one unlocks (you're now literally a 2-star hotel), and so on until done.
* Your goal (to "fulfill/beat" a star) is to **attract "stars"**, which are notorious high-prestige visitors (humans? aliens? animals?) of a certain levle.
* Which you do by fulfilling all their wishes, of course.

Could be **educational** if we include actual elements of OUTER SPACE. (Planets, rocket travel, meteor showers, oxygen needed, light from sun, etc)
