Incremental Boss Fight Game.

* Every "attempt" is just **another fight against one or more computer meeples**.
* You will lose many fights, but **gain stuff in doing so.**
* Upgrade to be faster, gain abilities, get more HP, unlock extra keys+powers (block/punch/area damage/etc), better shields, etcetera until you defeat them all.

So, the cycle is just repeated boss fights (that you will lose hopelessly over and over ... until you finally win).

**@IDEA:** The idea of health bar as a _grid_ is really interesting. (See: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6GE2kXiF1I0>.) In a way, you just replaced a health bar with "body parts" to hit, more of a spatial puzzle. Has loads of potential.
