Or some similar phrase related to the old **arcade games** (that required giving a coin each time you wanted to play).

An incremental game. What's the idea?

* Each attempt you get a large field of characters / machines / robots / etcetera.
* They do NOTHING.
* You have a little area ( = circle around mouse cursor) in which you can click characters, and a maximum supply of coins.
* **Click to put a coin into all robots within the area, so they will actually start doing work!**

The work itself can be anything. Mine resources, move stuff around, etcetera.

The unique core of the game is deciding _what_ to give your precious coins and optimizing that each time.

_I think this would work really well. It's just not the kind of game that I am interested in making right now._
