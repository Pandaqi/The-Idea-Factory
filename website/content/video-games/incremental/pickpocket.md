Incremental game, but pivoted more towards **extraction / roguelike / arcade** to make it unique.

Each run is a **busy market**.

> **You click anywhere (on any stall/person) to steal stuff from it.** 

Different stalls obviously give more/fewer/better rewards. You can play it safe and steal stuff close to the exits, or go for more valuable stalls near the center of the market.

What then?

* Owners will notice after _some time_. Watchful owners will are alerted more quickly / immediately of course.
* Stealing with police close by will alert them. 

How does police/capturing work? There are two "zones".

* DANGER: This zone automatically grows and starts from _anyone_ who could notice you or your thievery. You can _be_ inside this zone, but you can't _click_ inside it anymore.
* CAPTURE: This zone is made of static circles ("sight cones") around alerted police. You are immediately captured (and lose) if inside this zone.

The higher the alert level / the longer it goes on, the more police are asked to come, the more "specialized police" arrives (which may walk faster or have a higher sight range), etcetera.

The run ends when you are caught or you get away safely (which means reaching an exit OR the market closes).

Your end goal is to steal more and more money, so you can buy upgrades, so you can get the travel/pass/reputation needed to unlock the _next_ (bigger, more profitable) market.


**This could be offline/online multiplayer too!**

* You can work together by distracting guards, offloading loot, etcetera. For example: you steal something, you quickly throw it to the other player, and now when the police finds you ... they discover you have nothing on you and you are not captured.
* You can also play _against_ each other (a bit like "Good Friends Are Hard To Find"). Some are pickpockets, some are guards/stall owners, and you're trying to find/arrest each other.
