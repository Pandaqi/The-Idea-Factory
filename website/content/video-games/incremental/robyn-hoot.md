_I feel like the fantasy of playing Robin Hood is VERY STRONG and has general appeal, while there aren't many games about it._

ROBIN HOOD THE GAME --> **Robyn Hoot**, you're all owls and hooting noise is important and stuff :p

## Objective

Identify rich targets, research them, steal from them.

## Core Gameplay

After each theft, decide **how much you'll give back to the poor, and how much you'll keep yourself**.

* Giving back is needed to beat the game. It makes people happy, gives you REPUTATION/HEART resource, and maybe "helping someone out of poverty" this way unlocks new areas or makes them give you special powers.
* But you also need money yourself to buy upgrades and recharge yourself for the next attempt.

## Input

Gameplay happens entirely through BOW AND ARROW + CLICKING/CURSOR.
* You **alternate** between a killing arrow and a movement arrow.
  * AIM -> CLICK -> JUMP TO BALCONY
  * AIM -> CLICK -> SHOOT A GUARD
* This is how we can keep a single cursor for the entire game.
* The **hovering** can be a weaker effect, like "slowly weakening a door" or "slowly turning off the light"
