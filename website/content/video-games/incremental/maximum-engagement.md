BLURB: _You are the algorithm. Create maximum engagement._

An incremental/management game. Make decisions about what topics to support and which to push away, what to show and to how many people, with the only metric that matters being engagement/ads/money (and none of that stupid humany stuff).

In other words, you are the algorithm and you try to reach/influence more and more people every round. (Until you've influenced over half the world's population or something and beat the game.)
