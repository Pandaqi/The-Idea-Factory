What if people invented a weapon that was extremely powerful ... but due to its make (the bullets it needs, some special diamond at its core, whatever), it can't be close to any other weapons (or only weapons of the same kind). 

In other words, **you lead an army that can only wield one gun at a time**. 

Or, for more detail, which gun someone holds / which type of soldier they are creates an _avoidance radius_ around them that force pushes all other soldiers away. Now the player has to balance having the best/strongest/upgraded guns with being able to actually keep everyone together and in the right place.

This means that warfare suddenly changed to be very spread out, to optimize the number of guns per army, while always keeping in mind that the "primitive approach" with old pistols/knives/etcetera remains an option for an ambush.

_Could also be a STORY? What if a country invented such a weapon?_
