A tower defense game with only **ONE type of enemy and ONE type of tower and ONE type of attack**. 

Why?

* Saves effort with images and creating content.
* Prevents overwhelm and requires players to think in interesting new ways. 

What twist can we come up with to actually make this an interesting game? Because such an idea also requires the _developer_ to think in new ways ...

Extra Idea (Interesting): Just saw a game idea where you have to "fatten up" enemies before killing them. You have to purposely let them live a little longer so you can apply some other attack to them / let them grow more valuable, so you gain more from them when they finally die. 

* Basically you are rewarded for "cutting it close" as much as possible on all enemies.
* Maybe THAT is the answer: there's only one type of enemy/tower/etcetera, but their behavior/worth changes depending on LOCATION / HOW FAR ALONG THEY ARE ON THE PATH!


With this same name, by the way, you could also make a repeated-boss-fight-type-game.

* You start a game with a massive boss you cannot hope to defeat.
* But the more you damage them (before you die), the more coins/experience you get.
* So you try again and again and again (against the exact same enemy) ...
* ... until you finally win!
