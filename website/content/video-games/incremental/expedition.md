You could basically make an entire game just out of the Expedition system from _Two Point Museum_. 

* Each mission has events that might occur with a certain chance (and X events that will definitely occur).
* You send people from your squad on that mission + potentially add a rare/costly modifier or bonus to move things in your favor.
* Then they return from expedition, you get any rewards ( + see which exact events triggered of course), the people receive experience.
* Rinse and repeat.
