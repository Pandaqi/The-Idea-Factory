You play the _monster_ in typical video games. 

So there are several different game modes: tower defense, roguelite, arcade, boss fight, etc 

**You are part of the monster side of things, and you just try to do your job as well as possible.** Fit in. Don't die. Do your job admiradbly, don't overdo it. 

This gives you experience, you grow, you can buy "better" game modes at higher levels, and so forth.
