An experimental type of incremental game. An alternative name would be: _We Are (Not) Alone_.

The game just opens with a vast empty sky full of stars. 

You can earn money/experience/intel by **photographing/clicking on new stars and spaceships and stuff**. 

But it is _hard to see_. They're just tiny glowy dots from this distance, all the same. 

So, you can also **ZOOM IN/OUT**. 

* If we make it a 3D game, everything just being a `Sprite3D`, we can literally zoom you in/out to get a really nice effect.
* While zoomed in, you obviously know what's happening in that tiny area and have a much bigger cursor to click with. But you _can't_ see what's happening elsewhere.

Collect information and money, buy upgrades to be able to explore/see/understand more of the galaxy, and so forth.
