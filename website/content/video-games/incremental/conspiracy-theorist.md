**An incremental game where each "attempt" means DRAWING A LINE THROUGH STUFF.** 

Just a single line. Of a maximum length. (Upgrades obviously make it longer, more lines, etcetera). 

Try to thread the needle in such a way that you score the most points on that attempt. 

(Going through two things at once is bad? Being hit by moving monsters/dangers is bad? Maybe the requirements _shift_ so you need to constantly be on your toes?)

POSSIBLE THEME = **Conspiracy Theorist**. You have all these weird conspiracies on a cork board with pins, and you're connecting the pieces with red rope at each attempt. (Like that stereotype of people going mad when researching something, red wire crisscrossed and everywhere.)

_I think this idea could be great. I also think I'm not the right person to pull it off. Such a "controversial" theme requires a really committed and wacky execution, and that's not my expertise._
