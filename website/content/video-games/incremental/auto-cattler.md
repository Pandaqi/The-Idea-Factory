An auto battler version of Pokémon.

* The actual battles are _automated_ (so no more clicking the same attack a thousand times in a row etc.)
* Between battles, you go into nature to find new monsters (animals/cattle/pets/critters), to get more experience, to heal them, to evolve them, etcetera.
* Your decisions are about _what_ you put out into the field and how you've upgraded/tended to them in-between.

We can probably find a more SIMPLIFIED version of this too.
