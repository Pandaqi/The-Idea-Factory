An incremental game, ideal for Halloween, about ...

**all sorts of monsters and spirits escaping their coffin and you trying to put them back in :p** 

Click on the box to keep it closed. 

If someone already escaped, click to shove them in some direction, hopefully towards an open coffin to fall back into.

Basically, managing a graveyard, but with upgrades and funky monster/spirit/zombie powers.

_I think this could be really fun, but it's too small and so tightly connected to Halloween that I decided not to make it. I'd only make something like this as a one-week silly project to release for free near Halloween._
