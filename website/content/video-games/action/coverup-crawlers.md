There's something really interesting about [this prototype I saw](https://www.reddit.com/r/godot/comments/1relzmo/currently_at_the_thin_line_between_prototyping/) and the [update later](https://www.reddit.com/r/godot/comments/1rub9rn/experimenting_with_increasing_the_players_speed/).

A very self-contained arena (on a sort of half-dome, curving out), with some enemy/tower/radar in the center, and obstacles all around to hide behind. 

Simple movement of jumping around, hiding, then getting close to enemy when time is right to hit them.
