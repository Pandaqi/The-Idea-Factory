A tycoon-like management game about _becoming a superhero (or supervillain)_!

* You pick a minor power to start out. 
  * Bringing yourself into dangerous situations will grant you extra powers.
  * _Using_ your powers in different ways will give you experience and make them stronger.
* And then it's just about scanning your local area for events and reacting to them properly.
  * As you grow stronger, you can watch more of the city, move more quickly, get better info about the threat, etc.
  * The big thing is that you really need to be creative with your powers to solve the issues. **That's also the part, unfortunately, that will be extremely difficult to design and code.**
* And then you can have these messages/news events like "A new superhero/vigilante has come to town" or "Person X is at Level 3 on the path towards turning into a villain".

This feels like an exceptionally strong fantasy. But creating it will also likely be exceptionally difficult, unless I find great ways to simplify and streamline :p

For example:

* All superhero powers can be categorized into 4 or 6 main categories. This allows players to learn/remember the interactions between major categories, which is all that matters most of the time.
* All powers are "actions". They are attached to a _button_ that will, from now on, do that thing. Obvious example: "Superjump". Press A to jump really high at any time.
* All "dangers" are related to supervillains, which always have the exact same way to defeat them---they have a clear and communicated weakness or objective. The creativity of the game is about achieving that objective _with whatever power set you currently have_. Obvious example: knock the villain out.
* The "scanning for incidents and reacting" can be abstracted away into a very simple city map/overview, more like an incremental or puzzle game.
* OR we go the other way around completely and make it a big **freeform sandbox**. Your superhero "powers" can be literally anything. It copies/remembers your behavior or the exact circumstances in which you got the powers. (There are no "fixed powers" to choose from or whatever.)
