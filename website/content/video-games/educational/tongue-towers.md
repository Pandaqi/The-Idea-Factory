A sort of cooperative tower defense game using SPELLING/LETTERS.

**RULE 1:** Players ARE a letter. You can simply pick up/mine new ones, then bring/drop them somewhere else. ====> KILLED ENEMIES DROP NEW LETTERS! **This is also brilliant!**
 
**RULE 2:** _Connected straight lines_ of letters form a WALL. If it spells an existing word, the wall is much STRONGER.

**RULE 3:** Specific _Tetris-like shapes_ of letters, as long as they spell one existing word, lead to TOWERS. 

* Example: the shape could be "4 letters horiz, crossed with 3 letters at last vertical position"
* Example: if you create that shape with proper words, it turns into a tower.
* This may sound hard now, so let's add rule 4 ...

**RULE 4:** Towers allow you to drop in letters one by one (they only take up a _single grid space_; all letters stack up inside.) 

* Spelling _specific words_ leads to _specific weapons_. 
* (These are randomized per level. One level "CAT" might create a crossbow, another it might be "DOG".)

**RULE 5:** _Being_ a specific letter also changes things.

* Example: The "Z" makes you faster (as you zoom around the map), the "S" gives you a better shield, etcetera. (Also reinforce this with clear icons on all these letters, of course.)
* This is a way to make VERY UNCOMMON LETTERS still useful!

FUN BITS:
* The "treasure/gem/castle" you're defending is just a LIBRARY. 
* All your projectiles are just BOOKS or PAPER (WADS).
* There are these cartoony written sound effects alongside everything. (Using letters that are clearly much thinner and not interactable.)
