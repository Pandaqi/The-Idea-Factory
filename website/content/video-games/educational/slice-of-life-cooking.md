You are running a little restaurant, cafe, kitchen, whatever. 

You make a lot of pies and cakes and pizzas and pancakes => **Round stuff that needs to be sliced!**

In this incremental game,

* Each attempt has you **draw lines**.
* Those lines realistically **slice** whatever they pass through, creating smaller pieces.
* Smaller pieces score more at the end => once a piece is small enough, it scores instantly and removes itself

This is a really fun, simple, unique mechanic. I know how to slice 2D stuff from my work on Carving Pumpkins.

At the same time, the options for upgrades are endless.

* Longer **maximum length** of lines.
* Allow **more lines** in general. (Or quicker "reset" before doing your next line, if we go that route.)
* More pies, bigger pies, different types of pies, etcetera.
* A **target size** (like 1/8) that gives mega rewards. (**Could also be used educationally then!**)
  * Repurpose this to **clients** with **requests**. It's thematic and allows adding a larger variety of goals.
  * **Yes! We can make people request very SPECIFIC recipes, like 1/2 pizza and 1/2 pie.** If you merely **click** (instead of drawing a line), then you "combine everything within radius", and if this matches a customer's order then you score that.
  * This allows even more upgrades like number of customers, max difficulty/score of their requests, **click area size**, **mistakes allowed** (whether you may have wrong stuff in the combination too and it doesn't matter; this might be the default because it's more generous + you lose that stuff anyway so that's penalty enough)

Then we can **also** combine this with a kind of **tiered star system** with your kitchen's reputation. (Like the baby black hole -> bigger one -> etc progression in _A Game About Feeding A Black Hole_)

* You start with 0 stars and must earn X points in a single round to get 1 star.
* Once you have 1 star, more upgrades become available + there's a big global shift in gameplay (bigger map, one big RULE/MECHANIC explained)
* This continues until you have 5 stars and beat the game.
