A simple game centered on the idea of "owing somebody a favor".

On your turn, missions appear. These are ridiculously hard, on purpose, so you probably need other players to complete them.

* You either give away a **chip of yours** to another player to use their power/ability (on their character card).
* Or you cash in a **chip from somebody else** (that is lying in front of you). They owe you a favor and will thus help you.

When completing a mission, you get its points and any other bonuses. (_Examples: More tokens, retract some of your own tokens, forbid one favor, etcetera._)

You can also choose NOT to complete any missions on your turn. If so, you may retrieve **one** favor you gave out and do nothing else.

That's the game. Should work!
