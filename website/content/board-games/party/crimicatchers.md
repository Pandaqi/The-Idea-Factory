A simple card/party game about trying to catch the best piles of cards.

## Setup

Deal all players a unique cage card. Start!

## Gameplay

Played over multiple rounds. (4 or 5?)

Each round ...
-   Starts with X piles in the center of the table. (One faceup/facedown card per pile, in a row or grid.)
-   Players get 4 new cards in their hand.

### Turn

*Add* one of your hand cards to an open pile (facedown).

Each player has a **cage card.** When you decide to play it, put it **faceup** on an open pile. The pile is now *closed*, but it is *yours*! You're out of this round.

### End of Round

The round continues until everyone has claimed a pile. Now grab it and look at the content. Decide which cards you want to keep *in your hand* and which you want to *score permanently.*

## Objective

Cards *scored* give you points (depending on the card). Highest score wins.
