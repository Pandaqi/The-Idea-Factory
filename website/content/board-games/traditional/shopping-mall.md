A game in which all players run their own little shop ... inside the same shared shopping mall.

Everyone tries to earn the most money and prestige using their own shop. At the same time, because you're part of the same mall, you're heavily influenced by what others are doing / your neighbor stores. Decisions about events, sales, mall activities are _also_ made by everyone (like voting in a council).

You can expand your mall or try to start it at the most tactical location. You can buy more land to place a parking lot, leading to more customers for _everyone_, so you also need to make sure your shop is set up so that _you_ benefit the most.

You can choose to sell completely different things from everyone else ... or try to sell the same things but better, to steal their profit.
