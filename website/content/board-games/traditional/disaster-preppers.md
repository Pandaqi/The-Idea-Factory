A "Prepare For The Apocalypse" game, vaguely inspired by people preparing for emergency situations with covid and such.

The first half of the game, players are **preparing** and discovering supplies/finances/shelter/etcetera. 

At SOME POINT, someone will trigger the actual/specific disaster that happens.

And then it's just about **surviving for as long as possible** (with the things you prepped). 

Actually like that name, because it has double meaning (we're preparing for a disaster, but we're also _really bad preppers_).
