You're competing ice cream parlors on the beach, trying to serve tourists whatever their strange ice cream desires are.

## Setup

Create a deck of **Cone Tiles**. Shuffle and deal each player one.

Also create a deck of **Tourist Tiles**. Draw 4 and place them faceup in a row.

Finally, create a deck of **Scoop Tiles**. Draw 10 and place them faceup on the table.

## Objective

The game ends as soon as all tourists have been scored or have left. Each player sums the scores on their tourists: highest score wins!

## Gameplay

Take clockwise turns until done.

On your turn, **Scoop** and then **Score**. 

### Scoop

Pick scoop tiles from the center of the table. You must follow one of these rules:

* Either pick all scoops of the same _color_.
* Or all scoops with the same _icon_.

Then place these scoops on top of an ice cream cone. You must place _all_ scoops you have.

* These must be built like a pyramid (from bottom to top; complete one layer before starting another).
* Each ice cream can only have _one_ group per color. (In other words, you can't have two scoops of the same color that are disconnected.)
* If you place a scoop that matches _symbol_ with a neighbor, you immediately _stop_ placing scoops. (And can thus have some leftovers.)

You must place at least one scoop on one of your own cones. The others may also be placed on top of _other player's cones_!

Any scoops you simply _can't_ place return to the center of the table.

### Score

If you have the ice cream that a tourist wants, you may choose to score them. 

* Discard the ice cream (cone and scoops)
* Place the tourist on your score pile. (Some also have immediate bonuses you get now.)
* And draw a new one from the deck.

If there are no Scoop Tiles left at the end of your turn, do a **Refill**.

### Refill

Draw 10 new Scoop tiles and place them faceup on the table. All players also draw a new cone tile.

## To make this more interesting

First of all, there are four cones: size 1, 2, 3 and 4. (With the 3 being most common, as it's the most interesting.)

Scoops are placed on top like a _pyramid_. (For example, first row is 3, second row 2, top row 1.)

You can create **patterns** of matching scoops / sequences / symbols for bonus points.

Some scoops have _two_ sides. (They're split down the middle and show two colors. How you rotate them determines their use.)

Tourists might have other rewards besides points. (Draw an extra cone. Steal some scoops. Only this round, another tourist is 1 scoop cheaper.)
* Tourists generally want specific scoops (like 2xChocolate, 1xVanilla), and some scoops they surely do NOT want (like 1xStrawberry).
* They also have a cone preference. (Just show the 4 cones in order, but only highlight/make opaque the ones they want.)
* And sometimes a more specific preference. (No Chocolate next to Vanilla. No group that runs from top to bottom. The bottom layer can't be filled with only scoops of the same type.)

Special actions:
* Only need to place 1 scoop.
* Only draw at most 2 scoops.
* May swap/replace/place a scoop in a _hole_ (not a neat pyramid in order)

## Notes / Verdict

**Alternative names:** Scoop Center, Ice Dream Beach

Should work GREAT. 

Scoops might be actual circular tiles and cones triangle tiles. But also support square variants of these for easier cutting/more consistent deck dimensions.
