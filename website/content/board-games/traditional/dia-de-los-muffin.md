A game about **helping people on their journey towards the afterlife**. The title is a terrible twist on _Dia de los Muertos_.

-   You either have to give them certain things *before* they're able to "move on"
-   Or you have to make their journey as "nice and comfortable as possible"
-   Or you have to make sure they end up in the right place.

Anyway, the style of all cards and material should obviously be "Dia de los Muertos"-style. (Colorful, calavera, Mesoamerican, Mexican style.)
