A board game idea inspired by _Royal Game of Ur_.

You have a large circular playing board. (Several "rings" from outer to inner.)

Everyone **starts in their own section/pie** of that playing board, with a few pawns.

On your turn, you **roll a die and move ONE piece that many spaces**.

Your objective is to be the first to reach the finish line with ALL your pieces.

The catch? **Every ring has different rules about movement/capturing other players**.

* Outer ring = safe, can't capture
* Then you get a "capture" ring. (If you move on top of another piece, it is removed from the board and has to restart at outer ring.)
* Then you get a ring where movement is DOUBLED, or you simply SKIP used spaces.
* Then you get a ring where rolling a specific number is instant death or something. (And you _have_ to move exactly 1 piece on your turn, if you can, so that's how it can hurt you.)
* Etcetera

If a board with rings is too hard to do print-n-play, make it hexagons, or just a straight line, or something like that. The issue with "rings" is that they aren't variable: you can't say "for a faster game, only use 2" or "use any combination of 3 rings for random setup"
