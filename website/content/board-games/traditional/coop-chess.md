Could you play a game like **Chess**, but **cooperatively** (everyone working together, against the game)?

Everyone has control over 1 or 2 specific _piece types_, as you play against the _board_. 

(Or, if you have enough players, just a team-based thing.)

Alternatively,

* Everyone may move every pawn.
* HOWEVER, every player has a unique type of "movement" (2 steps straight, diagonal, L-shape, jump over stuff or not, etcetera.)
* Which means you need to coordinate WHO moves WHAT (and when) to reach your goals.
