Two types of material:
* Big, flat, rectangular tiles
* Folded paper---down the middle---to create 3D "fences" (or walls in this case).

You're building one or multiple Chinese walls during the game, trying to 

* Protect the lands behind it.
* Or control/enclose an area.
* Or prevent another player from visiting too easily,
* Or whatever.

Maybe the walls have colors/symbols, and you cannot connect walls of different types. Each player just tries to create the _longest_ possible wall. That's the unique thing about this game: over time, the table fills up with these beautiful long walls owned by different players. Because we use folded paper for the walls, we get this 3D aspect and you might even add a mechanic to walk _over_ these walls.
