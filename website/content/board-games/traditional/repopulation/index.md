This whole idea is based on a misunderstanding (by me) of the rules for "Captain of the Gulf". I like it.

## Setup

The board is a set of oceans and lakes. (Or one ocean divided into areas.) These are filled with **FISH** tokens. => These boards can be **randomly generated**, a printed A4.

Each player has one boat, which starts at any space on the edge.

## Gameplay

By default, you get 5 actions. However, for every 2 tokens currently on your ship, you lose 1 action. 

The possible actions are MOVE or FISH. At the end of your turn, always REPOPULATE.

### Move

Sail to an area adjacent to your current area.

The edges of the ocean have special locations. These trigger when you visit the area.
* Storehouse: load your cargo off your ship. (The tokens go from faceup in front of you, to facedown inside your score pile.)
* Shipyard: pay the tokens shown to buy an extra power. (These tokens may come from your ship or from your score pile)
	* Faster movement, may repopulate with any token, may fish the whole area even when it's not full, ...

### Fish

You can only fish in your current area.

You may _grab_ 1 token from the area, or _dump_ 1 token there (from your ship).

If there are **no** MINUS tokens, however,
* _All_ tokens inside the area are transferred to your ship. (Place them faceup in front of you.)
* Put back equally many **MINUS** tokens.

### Repopulate
In all areas, replace one **MINUS** token with a token of a type shown inside the area. (Some areas will always repopulate with fish, others can contain treasure, or trash, etcetera.)

## Expansions
I'd be tempted to move those "special locations" you can visit to an expansion, but I actually think they're crucial to the base game.

Similarly, there might be special AREAS. (A different rule is written _within_ the area.) Use one or two really easy ones for the base game, leave the harder ones for the expansion.
	* A max size for areas perhaps also makes sense.

Similarly, I'd like to add more tokens (besides FISH and MINUS), like trash/treasure/other stuff you might find in the sea. It would add complexity, but it would probably make the base game much better.

The more likely expansion would be WATER FLOW.
* During REPOPULATION: Move one token (can't be a minus) from each area to an adjacent area, following the arrows ("water flow").
* During MOVEMENT: Moving _against_ the water flow counts as moving 2 spaces.
