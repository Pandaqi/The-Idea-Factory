You have big chunky tiles with animal frames/bodies. And then you have lots of small tiles that you can _connect_ to those bodies to "build" animals (in a Spore-like fashion).

On your turn, you draft the body parts you want from some randomized market/tiles.

Then, once done, you actually _use_ the "pet" or "animal" you build for some common task in nature. 

* Such as gathering food
* Surviving in the wild
* Finding a mate
* Etc
