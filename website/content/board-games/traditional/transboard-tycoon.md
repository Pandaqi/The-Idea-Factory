Basically, turn the video game "Transport Tycoon" into a simplified board game.

Everyone starts with their own tiny, unconnected town. You start earning money by moving the few passengers/goods that are present in your town. This allows building longer roads and railroad tracks. You will connect to the cities of _other players_ over time, starting some trading and cooperation. Most resources on the map can only be claimed by _one_ person; most cities on the map can also only belong to the first person who connected to it.

In that sense, everyone is building towns across the whole country + infrastructure, _trying_ to eventually own the largest piece of the pie.
