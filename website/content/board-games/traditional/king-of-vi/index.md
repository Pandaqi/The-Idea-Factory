A **Viking-themed** boardgame. Below is a summary of the core idea, then a full rules description.

There are a few typical **Viking (long)boats** on the table.

* They contain **"slots"** (on top of the graphic or below) for Viking tokens.
* They are of varying lengths ( = varying number of slots)
  * (Some slots, especially later ones, can have extra benefits or actions when you place there.)
* And you can place an "Island" (or **"Destination"**) card at their front.
  * Cards already start with a "default island", both to simplify the game and to indicate this. Its rewards are meager, though, so you'll probably replace it with a new Island anyway.

Each turn, players will be **placing their tokens into empty slots**, trying to send the boats to the best islands and get rewards.

The Score Cards should just say something like.

* Resource = worth X points.
* Resource = worth the number of Viking icons you have left (colored or neutral)
* Resource = worth the number of boats you scored / slots on boats you scored
* Resource = worth 5 points if you have no resource Y, otherwise 1.


## Setup

Each player picks a unique Viking and gets all their tokens. (EXPANSION = + each player gets 3 random neutral tokens?)

Draw X **Boat Cards** and place them open on the table. 

Draw a random **Score Card** and place it in clear view of everyone.

## Objective

The game ends once all boats have sailed.

Your Score Card shows **how each resource scores**. Sum your points based on that. Highest score wins!

## Gameplay

From start player, take clockwise turns until done.

On your turn, **place a Viking token (of yours)**. Place it on a boat, in the first empty slot from the left.

* If you cover up an action slot, take that action.
* If you have the majority, you "own" the boat. You may change its Island card to a different one. (Optional: one from HAND, or MARKET, or within certain range of POINTS to prevent wild differences?)
* If the boat is full, it sets sail to its Island!

When a boat sails away, 

* Sort the players by how many Vikings they have inside this boat, highest to lowest.
* Its Island card tells you which rewards you get. (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, Other.)
* (When tied, both players get that same reward.)

Possible rewards are:
* More vikings (your color or neutral)
* The boat itself
* The Island itself
* Actual resource tokens (money, food, etc.)
