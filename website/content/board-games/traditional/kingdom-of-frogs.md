The vision is clear. 

* The board is a nice lake or river, beautiful, clear water, eye-catching.
* There are **water lilies** in the water.
* And players command **frogs** standing on those lilies, hopping around.

What are the actual game rules? There's one key rule, at least.

> **The water current constantly moves the water lilies towards the edge.**

As such, players need to constantly balance two things,

* Getting their frogs to the place they need (which gives them what they want/scores them the most points) and building little frog palaces there.
* But also GETTING OUT AGAIN before that lily has drifted off too much and is destroyed.

Other fun rules could be,

* Frogs can carry other frogs on their back. This way you can move _multiple frogs_ at once, but you _can't hop as far as usual_.
* You can "own" a lily, refusing passage to anyone else and scoring a lot of points from it. But you will completely lose it when it goes off the edge/out of the water.
