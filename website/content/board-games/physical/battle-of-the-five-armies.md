A physical game where you place and move soldiers by plugging them into actual holes (which make them stand up).

How?

* The board is a collection of cubes with openings on all sides.
* As such, if you place the cubes together, you get _holes_ (of different shapes) in the edges between them.
* These holes (only at the top??) can be used to plug in obstacles. 
* Otherwise, it just functions as a regular board and pieces can be placed anywhere you like.

Perhaps, if the holes aren't smooth enough, we can fill the holes we don't use with a wooden cylinder.
