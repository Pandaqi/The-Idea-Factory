The idea is to make a board game where **actual light and darkness matter**. 

* You turn off all the lights in the room, close the curtains, make sure it's quite dark.
* The game itself has all these electrical lights included => **tea lights** ("waxinelichtjes") most likely

Most of the gameplay is about **moving** and **turning on/off** those lights. You traverse some mysterious or dangerous tomb / basement / cave / whatever. Which parts of the map are lit up or visible is a huge part of the game.
