A game with a **big staircase** as the center piece. Like, a physical 3D staircase.

* All players are a pawn that starts all the way at the bottom.
* Different moves allow you to climb higher
* The first to reach the top wins

You can make this out of paper by folding/glueing strips of paper into _cubes_ (like creating dice). Cubes of different sizes, stacked on top of one another, create a stair-stepped pyramid that's sturdy enough to walk on.

Maybe more interesting rethemed as _mountain climbing_.
