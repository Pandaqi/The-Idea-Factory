An educational game about lobbying and being lobbied. 

Players can **assume powerful positions** where they make certain decisions.

At the same time, though, they can simply **offer money to other players** to make the decision THEY want. 

As such, to win, you need to find a balance between how much to push for _actually getting power_ (getting the role/power/position that gives you final say) or merely _bribing others in power to do what you want_.

That's a pretty pure simulation of how this actually works, and should clearly show why it's a BAD IDEA for the whole country.
