The map exists of **hexagon tiles**. (Same size, shape, general idea as the _Catan_ board.)

On their edges / corners are small empty spaces, perfect for placing tokens.

* When you combine **2 hexagons**, their long edge would be complete and allow a long rectangle token.
* When you combine **3 hexagons**, that point would now look like a triangle space, allowing a triangle token.

As the game progresses,

* Players expand the map with the big hexagons.
* While trying to claim the right spots (with their tokens in the right orientation) that appear between them.

Basically _Catan_, but with just a small set of tiles (nothing else), and hopefully way more variety and deep strategy.

This is somewhat similar to my _Elephant Lands_ idea, but advanced in a way that needs no randomly generated boards or weird elephant tokens trickery.
