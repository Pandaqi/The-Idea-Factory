A game about stopping a **rampaging monster** from destroying your pretty little village/city.

Can be **cooperative** (everyone against the monster(s)) or **competitive** (one or two players are the rampaging monsters, the other villagers).

This is the general idea.

## Villagers

* The villagers are building their village with tiles.
* They do so to ...
	* Get resources => these are needed to build more buildings, feed the village, etcetera
	* Lead the monster astray => simple tiles/walls/traps that force it to move in a certain direction.
	* Provide extra health/buffer

## Monster

* The monster walks around from tile to tile. 
* When playing cooperatively, the monster follows a simple ruleset.
	* It follows the arrows on the tiles.
	* If there are multiple paths, it picks the one that leads to the highest/juiciest reward.
	* OR: It must be _rotated_ first to follow a certain arrow? (So if there are multiple, it just follows the arrow with which it aligns. If there's no matching arrow, it rotates or does something special.)
	* **Anything the monster touches is destroyed.**
* When playing competitively, some of the same rules apply, but more of it is up to the _choice_ of the players playing it.
	* Multiple arrows? You pick one.
	* Special actions? You pick when/how to use them.

## Other Elements

The game could include a bunch of **monster tableaus** with special powers or stats (different health, different movement, etcetera)

## Objective

The game **ends** when ...

* The monster dies or is shut out. (Lost their health, or left the village with no entrance that isn't walled-off.) => VILLAGERS win.
* The whole village is destroyed or they can't feed themselves anymore. (No meaningful tiles still alive, or less food than there are people.)

_Inspired by Rhino Rampage._
