The idea is that you're traveling a route. You obviously try to **get to your destination first/fastest**, but you have an unforutunate obession with **following traffic rules at all times**.

* The major parts of the map are **traffic signs**. (Stop lights, speed bumps, intersections, one-way streets, etcetera.)
* These can be **changed**. For example, by placing a new tile over the old traffic light, you change it from red-orange-green.
* All game long, you're using cards to change the right traffic signs so _you_ can move forward, while _other players_ are stuck.

(Maybe you travel together, maybe individually. Maybe on bike, train, car, taxi, whatever. Bike seems fun for this, although they don't really follow most traffic rules. Use the octagonal grid (8-4) for this?)
