It's **SCRABBLE + DECK BUILDING + CARD GAME**

The idea is Scrabble / a word game / a spelling game ... but you hold **cards** in your hand.

-   Each card has one or multiple letters.
-   But also powerups/special actions.
-   Depending on how you rotate/order the cards, different letters are used, but also different powerups show.

Each round, you're constantly creating words and executing the actions you "created". (But only if you spelled a valid word, of course.)

After doing so, you lose some letters, and take the rest back. I want to make this akin to a "**deck building**" game. You need to manage your vowels and consonants, so you can keep writing cool words the whole game.

I'd like players to also write words **together**. So there are 1-3 words on the table. On your turn, you can remove letters from the start/end of a shared word, and add letters to the start/end. If you formed a valid word that way, you execute that action.

Actions:

-   Destroy a card from your deck
-   Add new cards to your deck
-   Steal cards from another player
-   One letter of your word is allowed to be incorrect? (It becomes a joker?)
-   You may play this word across two turns. (Next turn, you must finish it in a valid way, or suffer some penalty.)

The name _Keebble_ comes from my trio of language/Scrabble-inspired games called _The Keebble Games_. Not sure if this idea should be part of that. Alternative names are "Spelling Bee" => "Spelling Beast", or "Letterspells", or "Wordwizards"
