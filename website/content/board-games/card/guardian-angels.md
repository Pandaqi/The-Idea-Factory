The idea is that players are secretly linked: one as a regular being, one as their *guardian angel*.

But you don't know that. You need to figure that out through which cards you play.

How? Your "role" is assigned through the cards you have in your hand. => Would need to find a way to *ensure* all roles are distributed, and nobody has 0 or 2+ roles.

**Objective?** You win together. The angel must prevent bad stuff from happening to their partner, so they stay alive / score the most points when all cards have been played.
