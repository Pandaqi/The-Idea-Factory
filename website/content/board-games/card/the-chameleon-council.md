A game about attracting/using the right cards on and off the elusive Chameleon Council. Based on parts of the _"Scout"_ card game.

## Setup

Each player picks their unique **hero** card. Also deal each player 8 cards. 

Shuffle your cards and then take them into your hand. Because **from the moment you pick up your cards, you may not rearrange them anymore.**

## Objective

The game ends when somebody's **hero** is already on the council when their turn starts. In these rules, "council" simply refers to all cards currently faceup on the table.

Count the number of **crowns** on cards in your **score pile**. Subtract 1 crown for each card left in your hand. The player with the **most points wins.** 

## Gameplay

Take clockwise turns. On your turn, take one action: **Bribe**, **Hire** or **Activate**.

You can use any cards for these actions, except your **hero**. A player's **hero** can only Hire. If a hero were to be removed for whatever reason, it returns to the hand of the owner (at a position of choice).

### Bribe 

Steal a card from the current council and put it anywhere in your hand. Pay the owner a *card* of the same value or class.

### Hire

Play cards to the council. You can only ever play the _outer_ cards in your hand (left or right edge).

* For each card played, remove one card from the council that has a **lower value**. Add it to your facedown score pile.
* If you play multiple cards, they must all be the same value or class.

### Activate

Activate a card on the council, to take their action. Pay the owner a *card* of the same value or class.

## Card rules

Most characters have special rules/exceptions ...

-   When adding it
-   When removing it
-   When counting it as a point card
-   When certain pairings, combinations, or groups occur in the council
-   When activated => they do their special action, if they have one

This idea might also be repurposed to be about animals, for example. There's a hierarchy in the animal kingdom. They are predators, prey, plants, removing each other all the time. Would allow the characters to be actual *animals*. The *chameleon* is one of the more powerful and influential cards. Your main character is always a lion or king-like animal.
