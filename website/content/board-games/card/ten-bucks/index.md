## Setup

Create one deck of "dollar bills" and deal each player X (to take into their hand).

_The material for this game are just CARDS, perhaps DOMINO TILES, showing money in values 1--5._

## Objective

You win the game once you have claimed 10(+) money.

## Gameplay

On your turn, **play one dollar bill** from your hand to the table. Dollars with the same value are stacked. (Example: if there's already a 2-dollar bill on the table, you place your 2-dollar bill on top of that.)

Then you're allowed to **claim a stack**. Take one stack ( = all money of the same value) and place it before you.

There are, however, three rules to claiming.

* You **can't claim** a dollar value you claimed before, nor the value you just played.
* If you create a stack that's **worth 5+ dollar** on its own (summed), you must force someone (which can be yourself) to claim instantly.
* You can never be more than 1 stack ahead of the player with the fewest stacks. (In practice, this means you have to _wait until all other players have claimed too_ before you can claim again.)

> Example: one player hasn't claimed anything yet. You already have 1 stack. Then you can't claim a 2nd one until that weakest player has claimed something.
