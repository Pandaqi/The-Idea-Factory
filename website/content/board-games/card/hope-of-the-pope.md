Werewolf-like game, but this one is about **choosing the next pope**!

Every player has one or more specific **secret missions**. (Example: "you must be the third favorite OR player X must vote for you twice this game")

Every round, you **vote on who should be pope**. 

* If there's a 2/3 majority, they are picked and the game ends. 
* If not, the one with the _fewest_ votes is now **out of the running** and we play the next round. 

What does "out of the running" mean? They can't become pope anymore, BUT they still continue playing, voting, etcetera as they may still _win the game_.

If this continues for a bit, you are guaranteed to pick a pope at some point.

There are **special powers/actions called "sins"**. 

* You can "sin" to get that action, which yields information, makes your win condition easier, etcetera. 
* But when you do so, your "holy/clean/pure cards" before you are replaced by that sin card. 
* If someone accuses you of sinning, and you did, they are rewarded and you are punished ( = can never become pope anymore?)
