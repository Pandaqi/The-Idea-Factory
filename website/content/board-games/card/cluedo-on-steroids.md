Everyone is locked into the same location together. (A spaceship? A manor? University?)

The game has two phases:

* **Before** the murder
* **After** the murder

All players heard the rumor that a murder will take place tonight. Every player has the opportunity to actually _be the murderer_.

Once the murder has taken place, though, that murderer now needs to escape this constricted shared location before the other players catch them.

How does this work specifically?

* In the first phase, players move around, swap cards, and so forth trying to get the _ideal situation to commit the murder_.
* Because once you do it, you leave behind _evidence_ (depending on where and how, using which cards, etc)
* The other players will slowly unravel that evidence, building a "solid case".
* And once they can name all the properties of your murder (location, weapon, etc) AND figure out where you are right now, they catch you.
