Cooperative "one paper game". Everyone is on an island (randomly generated by the computer of course) and your only goal is to *survive* until you can *leave* (ending the game).

## What's unique?

* There's an actual secret/mystery you need to solve to get off the island. (Maybe even multiple ones; each one opens a treasure, or door, or whatever.)
* This depends on the SEED for the game. To *check* if you're correct, you need to input your board seed, and then the solution on the website.
* **IDEA 2:** Each player gets their own *backpack*. This is randomly generated by the computer and contains items that you *somehow* need to get off the island. It's a small square/rectangle that you can cut off the board and hand to each individual player.
* **IDEA 3:** some *rules* are also different between games? For example, one game might say: "traveling across dirt roads costs 2 time", and another "traveling across dirt roads is free, but you take one damage"

## What's the challenge?

Find a balance between *survival* and *finding a way to leave the island*. (Because you only have limited time and resources.)

## What do I want from this game?

-   Simultaneous play => just way faster, supports more players, keeps everyone more engaged. (This would mean there is a *timer* on this game and everyone acts simultaneously? Are players somehow *separated* both geographically and functionally, so they will never directly interfere with their actions?)
-   Beautiful maps => nature, varied environments, logical places for survival (e.g. shelter can be found in rock formations)
-   Practical rules => rules must be inspired by actual, practical survival tips. (Maybe even make notes/remarks about this in the rules.)
