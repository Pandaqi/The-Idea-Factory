Every player runs their own post office / mailing service / package service.

* You are trying to send mail with messages over the board. Think of connecting/filling routes like in _Ticket to Ride_.
* It would be fun if you actually had tiny envelopes, and could write ANYTHING on a message and then put it inside that.
* You are punished for packages that don't arrive, or arrive too late, or damaged.

## As a Logic Puzzle

This could also be a **logic puzzle** like Sudoku and such, just with more theme.

* You are given a random road network and stuff on paper.
* You are given some mail with random destinations
* (There might be some input randomness as to what vehicle you must use, or where you must turn, or SOMETHING)

Your goal is to **draw lines** (following a few logical rules) to create the **entire path to deliver all the mail**.
