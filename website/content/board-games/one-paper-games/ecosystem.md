A _One Paper Game_ about making the **prettiest and most balanced ecosystem**. You print out the paper and play by writing on it with a pen(cil).

For example,

* There must be twice as much prey a predators.
* There must be twice as many plants as prey.

Could also be more like a _logic puzzle_ by having some Sudoku-like extra rules and special effects of animals ("only eats animal X", "counts for 2 predators if Y"). This would _dictate_ how exactly to fill up the map, instead of allowing randomness / creative freedom.

IDEA: Something about invasive grasses, or species, or just the threat of humans. And you have to prevent that?
