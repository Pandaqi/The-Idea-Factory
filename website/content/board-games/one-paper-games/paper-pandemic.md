A _One Paper Game_ about either PROTECTING against a spreadin virus, or SPREADING the virus yourself. You print out the paper and play by writing on it with a pen(cil).

The paper shows a randomly generated country/map/population.

It's a solo game, more like a logic puzzle, that you play against this random map.

After every turn of yours, the virus automatically spreads to adjacent cells following some rules.

You simply have to save X people, or _someone_, or kill the virus entirely (as the hardest difficulty)
