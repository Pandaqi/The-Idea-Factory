A _One Paper Game_ that you play solo / against yourself. Print a random paper, grab a **pen** and **die**, and play.

## Objective

The game ends as soon as you _can't_ execute your turn anymore. Calculate your score and check it against the target score(s).

## Gameplay

Roll the die. Its **number** tells you **which terrain element** to place. Every cell on the map can only be filled _once_.

Different terrain has different rules about placement and scoring.

Also take into account the rules of existing elements, when you draw something there. These only appear on harder difficulties.

## Terminology

* "Adjacent" always means directly to its left, right, top, or bottom---never diagonally.
* All adjacent tiles of the same terrain are considered one "group", which is often important in this game.
  * A group of (adjacent) Trees is called a Forest.
  * A group of (adjacent) Water is called a River.
  * A group of (adjacent) Towns is called a City.
  * A group of (adjacent) Grain is called a Farm.
* The sun icon around the edge indicates the "Sunlight Direction". Any tile behind a mountain, on the _opposite_ side of the Sunlight Direction, is said to be "in shadow".

## Map / Paper

A large empty grid, but with several features and special elements already filled in.

### Map Modifications

* **Existing Terrain**: the easiest one, and the only one at the start. Some terrain is already filled in, which gives a nice starting point + random restrictions already.
* **Point Change**: simply adds/removes points, or doubles/halves.
* **Terrain Options**: Forbidden/Required Terrain
* **Disable Rules**: disables the usual rules for placement/scoring for whatever you place here.
* **Pick Next**: next turn, pick your terrain yourself, instead of rolling the die.
* **Min/Max Group Size**: at time of placement, the terrain must be part of a group below/above the given size
* **Relic Capture**: a special location. If surrounded completely by terrains, you capture the Relic ... which gives some neat bonusses/moves you along a track? (At the very least, place 1 or 2 terrains of choice immediately?)

### Terrains

* **Water**: must be placed adjacent to Water, but can only be connected to 2 Water tiles at most. You score the length of your longest river. Any city connected to another city through your longest river has its points _doubled_.
* **Grass**: can be placed anywhere. Scores +1 point.
* **Tree**: must be placed adjacent to a Tree, _if possible_. Scores +2 points, but only if part of a Forest of size 6(+).
* **Town**: must be placed adjacent to Water or Grain. Scores 0 points by itself, but +1 point for every adjacent Town.
* **Mountains**: no part of the map may ever be completely encircled or cut off by mountains. Only Water/Grass is allowed in a mountain's shadow. Scores +1 point.
* **Grain**: can only be adjacent to a City with a _smaller_ size than its Farm. Scores +2 points.
