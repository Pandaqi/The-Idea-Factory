While you play, a "history" is recorded of all your actions, where you were at certain times, etc.

The idea is that you can travel forward/backward in time to do crazy tactical maneuvers and win the game.

Possible specific ways to do this:

* Lay cards in sequence (left-\>right). Things that happen "at the same time" are laid in the other direction (top-\>bottom). So it's obvious what the timeline is. Now you can always go back to a previous card.
* OR; Make this a *One Paper Game*, which automatically records what you did and when. (Because you write it on the board, and those writings are permanent, you always get a history of all you've done.)
  * When you *switch* a timeline, or create a *parallel one*, or *destroy one* ... you can just change papers! Grab a new paper, remove the old, fold/cut the conflicting part, whatever.
