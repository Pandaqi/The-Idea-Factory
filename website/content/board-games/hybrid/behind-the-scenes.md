A hybrid board game.

When the game starts, there are no specific rules or actions. Instead, on your turn, the *smartphone* gives a list of actions you can currently do! You simply pick one or execute one.

(If possible, one could also require *all players* to use their phone. This way, they can see their actions in advance and plan for it. Saves time.)

The **goal** of the game can be anything, but the phone represents someone whispering instructions **behind the scenes**. This lends itself best for a kind of improvisation party game about reacting to prompts or trying to convey a secret word or something.
