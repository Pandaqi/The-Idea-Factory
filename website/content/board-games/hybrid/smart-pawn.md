A game where the playing board is "analog" (a printed paper, bunch of tiles, something physical and tangible).

But **each player's pawn is their smart phone**. They place their phone on this playing board and move it around following certain rules. Phone = pawn.

Your pawn ( = phone) also indicates what you can and can't do.

-   Maybe there are arrows pointing at sides to show you can move there, or forbid others from moving there.
-   There are multiple icons (+ descriptions?) for special powers
-   Maybe there are "logic rules" you must obey.
-   Or there are "resources" on your phone you can spend. (Or you must have at least X of a certain type, before that ability unlocks in your pawn.)
