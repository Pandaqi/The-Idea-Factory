A family business that grows larger and larger and larger (inspired by Ferrero / Nutella story). The parents have three children, and they follow the simple rule that the **oldest inherits the business**, and the **younger ones are encouraged to become helpers** but not required. 

Then ... in a suspicious accident/event ... both the owner and the oldest child are killed. Everyone starts reaching out to the second sibling, but they have mysteriously vanished. **And so the youngest one---who was never trained for this and had accepted they never had anything to do with the company---is suddenly asked to lead the company.**

This can be ...
* A comedy kind of thing.
* Or it can be a thriller/cozy crime, as they try to figure out who inside the company would kill the owner/oldest child, and where the other one vanished to. (While trying to save the company and learn on the go.) 
  * -> It seems obvious to me that someone would _want_ the inexperienced child to lead, and then, throughout the entire story, they whisper the wrong advice in their ear and stuff. 
  * -> So the murderer would be his closest assistant who seems a friend and really helps him out at the start, until (s)he realizes certain advice they gave was VERY WRONG and they KNEW IT.
* Maybe there's some TERRIBLE SECRET hidden in the company's past/present. Like slave labour, unethical sourcing of materials, lying about what's inside the chocolate, a hidden branch doing shady stuff to competitors, etcetera. 
  * => That's why the second sibling had NO interest taking over and they now have to struggle with this new information and hiding it/exposing it in the right way.
