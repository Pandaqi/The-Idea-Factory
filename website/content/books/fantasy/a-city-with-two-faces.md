Takes place in a very unusual city.

There's a "day side", which has your usual shops, for humans, typical stuff. 

And there's a "night side", where stores have to change, and they open up to some back part or other part of town. 

It's always been that way---because at night, creatures visit and want to use the city too, and you'll be punished if you don't help (and/or they're just good clients and you don't want to miss out). 

As such, the city has two completely different parts/faces/functionalities ... until, of course, things go wrong and the parts are forced to clash/mix.
