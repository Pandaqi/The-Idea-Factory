Someone finds a way to listen to radio broadcasts from some place he's never heard of. They're really interesting, giving hints about a place that's magical, and different, and beautiful. 

The person _longs_ for this world oh so much. They start finding letters from people who live there too. Snippets of messages, stories, images, etcetera. 

They become obsessed with _receiving_ everything from Nowhereland, and then of course _finding it_. 

And then, in the end, it's ambiguous whether it ever existed at all or not. The story is more about _longing_ and _fantasy_---about how someone managed to live their life, be happy, be content---by just enjoying the stories from "some beautiful land far away" every night.
