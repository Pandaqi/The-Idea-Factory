A world that has a kind of middle ground between magic and reality, between magic and science/invention.

* You _can_ achieve a lot of things that would be Fantasy, done through magic and spells in other stories.
* But then you must first, with blood and tears, invent a **device** for that specific purpose and build it.
* In that same way, you can get a lot of power and magic by being able to _buy_ these devices from other wizard inventors. (Lots of money, lots of connections, knowing the right people.)

This is inspired by the idea of **fabrials** in Brandon Sanderon's _Stormlight Archive_.

The plot/story could be something like:

* One character is a _student_ trying to learn from their master and invent their own machine. (But the master is understandably secretive and paranoid.)
* One character who works in a kind of "underground machine shop". It's illegal, but they have lots of clients buying their magic devices, and they're quite rich and powerful in that way.
* One character who is clearly abusing this power. (Or legends say he cracked the "magimachine code" and made a machine that can invent other magical machines on its own => infinite power glitch)
* One character who is desperately _looking_ for a specific magic machine (to heal their sick parents, to pull off a bank heist, whatever is important to them)

We could **combine** this with that idea from _The Last Wish_ (movie).

* Everyone knows a superduper important object (plant/animal/stone/whatever) exploded away once upon a time and landed in a dark forest far away.
* Could be an accident, a spell, a curse, a consequence of some historic event, it does not matter.
* The point is: **everyone wants that thing**. It's common knowledge that _it exists_ and it _allows creating lots of magic_, but ...
* The dark forest is dangerous. Everyone keeps organizing missions going into the dark forest, trying to get inside in different ways (underground tunnel, dropped from a plane, etc) But nobody returns alive. Or they return ... "changed". 

It's a simple way to both have a clear goal _and_ get a lot of mystery and tension for free.
