A world in which everything you take from the earth (plants, raw materials, space) is registered and tracked as part of your **Dirt Debt**.

(Inspired by how the "debt" of all countries in the world is constantly rising. But to whom is that debt? Nobody. It's from wealth acquired by growing stuff in the dirt, claiming space, etcetera. So it's basically a debt to _earth itself_.)

This debt determines what you can or can't do. You _must_ pay back the debt, and if you can't do it any other way, it has to be by dying and giving yourself back to the earth.

**@VERDICT: A very promising, unique, evocative idea---but what's the story?**


**@IDEA (STORY)**: A world in which EVERYTHING is tracked. As in, they figured out how to modify molecules or whatever to track them everywhere, so you can find the exact origin and path of EVERYTHING if you "read it out" or something.
