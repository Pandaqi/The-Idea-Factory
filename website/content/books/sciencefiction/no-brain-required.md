A story about someone who just cannot choose, so he decides **to let AI make _all_ decisions for his life.**

This starts with the obvious big, hard choices (should I go to university? should I take that job?), but becomes more and more narrow.

Until the AI basically decides _everything_ for that person and they never question any of its statements ever. Which obviously makes them feel hollow, leads to issues and silly situations, etcetera.
