A picture book about an _alchemist_: someone who knows a lot about plants, science, potions, and more. She uses all that to create all sorts of wonderful magical potions that do silly things ... but exactly what she needs at the moment to solve a problem.

**Interactive?**

* The book gives clear colors/icons/names for all the different ingredients and potions.
* Because it provides little "puzzles" all the time asking children to find the right combinations and "invent" the potion themselves!
* Perhaps there could be some companion website in which you can enter your potions and see what comes out.
* (That would be a more simplified, cute version of _Wondering Witches_. With simpler shapes and softer colors, yet a much larger palette of ingredients.)

Note: The original Dutch title of this idea was _Alchemist Achjee_.
