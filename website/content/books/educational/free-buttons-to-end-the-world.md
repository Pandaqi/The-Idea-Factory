_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3/4, Topics Wars & Geopolitics._

A planet where every country is given one **"nuclear weapon"** (or some magical equivalent of it) and a **button** they can press to activate it. Exactly one, no more and no less. 

It's also completely forbidden to **develop weapons in any way** besides that. 

Why? Because of the "standoff"-effect -> because EVERYONE has that button, pressing it means mutually assured destruction, which they hope is enough to prevent anyone from ever actually using it and achieving world peace. 

> "It turns out that achieving world peace was _easy_! Keeping it, however, was ... much harder."
