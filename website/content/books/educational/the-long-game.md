A story about a **worldwide huge lottery** ... that's been **going on for centuries**. By now, entire families have thousands of lottery numbers, there's a huge history, there are people trying to crack the algorithm/safe house and find the one number that's been drawn, etcetera. Basically a lottery on steroids, dramatized, whimsical, huge.

_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, teaching about probability, gambling, etcetera._
