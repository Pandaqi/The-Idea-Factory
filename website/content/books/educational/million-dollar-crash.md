_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 2, Topics Money._

Because the streets/railroad hasn't been safe for centuries, a country moves all its cash (and gold/valuables/whatever) by plane. Until one day ... a huge plane with trillions of dollars **crashes in an open field**.

Immediately, people try to grab all the money and get away. They try to contain the news to prevent others from coming. The police tries to _stop_ citizens from grabbing the money, but fails when the crowd gets too big and fighting breaks out.

Lots of potential for story from a single simple inciting incident.

Also, could be a _game_. You start with a "random" crash and scattering of the money. Now players try to move around the board and grab as much money as they can before police has arrived in large enough numbers / all of it is gone. (Board game or Video game would both work.)

Inspired by this Dutch article about exactly such an event: <https://nos.nl/artikel/2604305-vrachtvliegtuig-met-bankbiljetten-crasht-in-bolivia>
