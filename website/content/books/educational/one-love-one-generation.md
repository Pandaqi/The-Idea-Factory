_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Biology & Ancestry._

**What if there was only ever _one_ generation?** 

That generation lives their entire life, then they **all die**, and the _next_ generation is born from the seeds they sowed/whatever they did and left behind. 

Maybe you can literally "get pregnant" now ... but the child will only be born _next generation_.

This is a VERY INTERESTING way for biology or a world to work and can open up deep stories about intergenerational trauma, importance of having parents, generations, etc.
