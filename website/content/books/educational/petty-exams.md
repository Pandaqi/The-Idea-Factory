_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Regulations, Exams & Pets._

A world where you have to **pass an exam, some rigorous testing and stuff, before you're allowed to get a pet.** 

Really interesting ... because we might actually want to have something like this in the real world in the future. 

And it opens the door to teaching about pets + exams, maybe superhero pets, etc
