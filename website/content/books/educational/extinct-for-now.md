_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Biology & Nature._

**What if a country figures out how to bring extinct species back?** 

More specifically: how to take DNA and create the living organism from it again.

What would they choose? In what order?

The only way to do this is probably _slowly_, as naturally as possible, which means it takes a lot of time and resources and is very risky. This probably dampens their speed, the species they _can_ bring back, but it also gives rich/powerful people control and a status symbol ("look, I could pay to bring back a DINOSAUR").

Maybe some old species are _easy_, so now we have an overabundance of them and they do _too well_ in the current habitat. Other old species do _even worse_ in the current habitat.

Making this a "science breakthrough" or "ingrained part of society" feels interesting for story.
