_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Privacy & Surveillance._

A world where these thin cloths/drapes/nets float and twist and twirl through the air everywhere. 

> Like really thin dementors, or those flat pancake fishes in the sea. 

The people call them "Ghosts" or "Spirit Blankets" or whatever.

This is not only a mesmerizing sight, they also have a function. Like, they might give off light and give first aid if needed, they patrol neighborhoods, they create pockets of magic, government uses them to spy, whatever.
