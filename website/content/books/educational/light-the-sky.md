_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Companies, Capitalism & Monopolies._

**A world where a company managed to _monopolize_ the sun, and thus make solar energy scarce and all.**

Or maybe this is unknown. It's just "something changed to the sun long ago and now all of us live in darkness and only THAT COMPANY can attract sunlight/the sun's attention and generate solar energy"

It's about unearthing that mystery and fighting to get lights in the sky again. Fighting to allow everyone to use the sun for free again. Creating pretty lights even against all odds and people giving up because it's just "always dark, always has been, always will be"
