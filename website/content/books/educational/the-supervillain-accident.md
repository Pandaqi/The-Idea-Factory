_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Technology & Responsibility._

**About someone who's a "supervillain" ... but by accident.** 

They're just quite smart, and curious, and motivated to figure things out. So they _accidentally_ start developing some really dangerous weapon, or really smart AI, or scientific experiment, that ends up destroying half the world. 

Which is INTERESTING because it's a story against a truly scary villain ... who isn't evil in any way.
