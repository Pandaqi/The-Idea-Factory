_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 4, Topics Culture, Society & Criminality._

About a "rough neighborhood", with kids slowly being radicalized to become more extreme/join a gang. 

But there are one or more people in that neighborhood who actually look out for them and keep them out of gangs/trouble. 

They think it's just coincidence and that those are good people ... until they learn the police purposely _planted_ them there because it'd be much more effective and controlling the situation than putting police officers in. 

This causes them to believe their entire relationship/friendship has always been a "lie" and makes them do something stupid in the moment.

Include the idea of "Gang Databases" from the _Last Week Tonight_ segment.
