_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Business & Economics._

**A country where it's illegal to run a "company" or "business".** 

You're _always_ individuals, responsible for your own actions, etcetera. Larger projects mean you just happen to be working together right now/choosing to do X together/work in the same place. 

You can _never_ hide behind shared responsibility, or a company name, or a boss/manager saying you had to do something, etcetera. 

Alternative title = Corpalone.
