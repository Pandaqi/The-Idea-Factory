_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Cooperation & Politics._

**"Coalition of the Willing"**, it's just such a nice phrase for a story or game or whatever. 

"Coalition of the Cooperatives" or "Faction of the Fighters For Good" or something like that, which consists of people with no discernible skill or power---their hands are tied, they can't do much, all odds stacked against them---except for that they are _willing_ to try, to forgive, to work hard, to create something better.

> "Well, they don't call us the Coalition of the Capable, do they?"
