_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, but I could not find a clear topic or use for it._

It's a secret organization that works to prevent anyone/anything from becoming _too big_. From becoming _extreme_. 

Once a company gets too large, they'll start annoying it and destroying it to cut it down. 

When someone gets too wealthy, they'll fight against it. 

The story is a mystery about _what_ on earth is happening---and then, as soon as this squad is revealed, we get a second viewpoint from the squad itself and their missions.

(Might even start with someone who is "helping" them, but without knowing who they are. They just get messages like "give us information X" or "be in place Y at 8 o'clock if you hate your boss".)
