_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Nature & Intelligence._

**A world where suddenly all animals get "human-like intelligence".** 

But they keep their general body, personality, etcetera. 

* Some animals would probably try to get revenge on humans. 
* Others will try to _protect_ the humans that have been kind to them. 
* Some will simply go for independence, disrupting our food industry and stuff.
