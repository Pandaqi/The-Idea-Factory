_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Government & Politics._

In a **heavily corrupt country** (/island/empire), a certain family **suddenly finds themselves in power**. A complete surprise. Weird turn of events.

They are stuck between two minds: 

* Either get away safely right now
* Or stick around and actually try to change the country for the better

They are outsiders with little support, only trusting their own family as guards and in the powerful positions. (The reason they get into power can be a bit silly / foolish, especially if the story is for young kids, as this provides an interesting funny first chapter.)
