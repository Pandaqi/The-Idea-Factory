_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Biology & Climate._

**A country decides to "absolutely ruin" all the nature and areas at their border**.

Why? To make it completely impossible for anyone to invade (and/or migrate to it?) 

They set stuff on fire, release toxic gasses, let the biggest predators run wild, etcetera. 

They do it in such a way that _they_ are not harmed by it---they have merely permanently lost some land. It just becomes this impossible wasteland of death for anyone else to trek through.

Maybe, though, it's revealed that there _is_ a way to get in and out of the country (because president needs it, people want it, migration was still desired) ... and that turns out to be a weak point that another country tries to exploit?
