_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Fire / Elements._

It's about a world where **life only continues as long as one (or several) Holy Fires keep burning**. So when they are threatened, for whatever reason, all people scramble to save those fires.

This can be extended to _all_ elements then. There are different tribes/groups/institutions that "manage" or "keep" a specific element. Not only do they need to keep it alive, they need to keep creating enough of it + you must travel by them (or they come to you) to _get_ it. 

So, if you need a fire, you might need to wait until the Firekeepers swing by next week. If you need water, there is a single place where hundreds of people work really hard to _create_ it. If you need soil/dirt, same thing. Air, same thing. It's all "commoditized", but in a magical and usable way.

Alternative Names = "Endless Fire" or "Keepers of Fire" or "The Fire Carrier"
