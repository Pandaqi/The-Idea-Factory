_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Government & Politics._

**A world in which _every position of government_ is directly chosen.** 

This means the president can be from a completely different party/part of the spectrum than the vice-president, and now they have to work together. 

This is at least interesting for a story, if not an actually usable system.

In a _board game_, this would mean 

* Laying out a set of cards with those roles (in order: president, vice-president, minister of X, etc)
* And then people vote, and they are sorted by votes and assigned those roles.
* And depending on your personal party alliance / goals / actions you want, this can be good or not.
