_This idea was originally meant for my educational online store, Level 3, Topics Mindset & Self-Development._

What if you had a world where **what the majority of people believed BECAME true**? 

Like, manifestation made magic---we can literally call it "Manifest" or "Manifestic". (Alternative titles = Behold & Become, or Beholders.)

Reality changes all the time to what most people _believe_ it should be. 

Interesting.
